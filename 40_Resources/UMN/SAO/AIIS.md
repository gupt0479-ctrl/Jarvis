# AIIS Club
Part of a club for managing the finances of this club. 
## What AIIS Is
AIIS is a University of Minnesota - Twin Cities AI-focused student group (run out of Carlson, given the cross-collabs with Carlson clubs). It is not yet a formal statement of mission/purpose - the [[#AIIS Constitution]] (governance doc with the official mission statement, needed verbatim for Proposal Question 3) is not accessible from this Drive account, so this section is built from the two docs I could read.
Fall semester runs ==7-8 meetings/events, 6:30-8:00 PM==, split across two rooms, plus a flagship hackathon planned for spring:
1. Kickoff event - Sept 16
2. Fun AI Social Event - Sept 30 (possible collab w/ Robotics Club or SVP; gamified AI concepts; prompt engineering competition)
3. Fall AI Convention - Oct 1 (see below)
4. Unsupervised Learning & Transformer Architecture Night (industry technical speaker)
5. GTM Strategy Night (collab w/ Carlson clubs CCP, B4G)
6. AI Innovation Shark Tank Night (industry judges)
7. AIIS Panel Night
8. Chill Study Night / Networking event
Spring semester: flagship hackathon(s) - not yet detailed.
### Fall AI Convention (Oct 1) - the event most likely to become the SAO Grant proposal
- Theme: "Using AI to cultivate joy / a fulfilling life"
- Joint event with Nexus (another AI club) - 25 students each side, 50 total
- Venue: Anderson Student Center, 2115 Summit Ave, St Paul - reserved 5-11 PM, event runs 6-9 PM
- Format: Dinner/networking (1 hr) -> Panel moderated by Amal + Sophia (1 hr) -> "The Launchpad" pitch competition, audience vote + panelist feedback (1 hr)
- Prize pool: AIIS contributes $300 ($150/1st, split for 2nd/3rd)
- Panelists (5 target): Dr. Keisier, Anushka Bhide, Darsh Garg, Norman Owens, Jacob Brindley
- Sophia owns budget by Sept 10; panelist confirmations also due Sept 10
- This is a joint-group, ticketed-prize event - worth checking against [[Pre-Application - Event and Expense Eligibility]] (prizes/giveaways are generally **not** eligible for SAO funding) and [[Event Proposal - Questions 8 & 9]] (Q8 partnership responsibilities is mandatory here since Nexus is a co-host)
