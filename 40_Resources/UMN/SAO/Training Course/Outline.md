# Course Outline
## Objectives
As a result of this training course, you will be equipped to:
- Find resources to assist your group in applying for SAO Grants
- Understand what a complete SAO Grants Proposal includes
- Know which expenses are unallowable to be funded by SAO Grants
- Understand how to complete the necessary Grant application steps before, during, and after your event
