# Event Venue
## One-Line Answer
Check a venue's ==accessibility, technical needs, and reservation lead time== before booking, and have a backup plan if your activity doesn't fit the space (like Gopher Cooking Club's kitchen swap).
## Event Planning: Venue
Finding the right space is one of the most important steps in the event planning process. The University of Minnesota Twin Cities campus has dozens of reservable venues and spaces available to registered student groups, including theaters, athletic facilities, meeting rooms, reception areas, outdoor spaces, and classrooms. In general, [Conference and Event ServicesLinks to an external site.](https://uces.umn.edu/) is a great place to start when looking for venue and space resources.

---
## Before You Reserve - What Are Your Needs?
Once your group has identified a venue budget, determine what reservable spaces will best meet your event needs. Consider the following:
- What atmosphere is needed for your event?
- What size room do you need?
- What audio/visual or other technology needs do you have?
- What have you budgeted for venue and technology costs?

---
## Major Events
A [Major EventLinks to an external site.](https://policy.umn.edu/operations/majorevents) is an event on campus that has the potential for large crowds, media involvement, and/or high-profile speakers or performers. For the safety of your group, performers, attendees, and the broader community, the University may require enhanced security, emergency personnel, or specific precautions. If you are planning a large-scale event, review all relevant University policies. The Student Activities Office can help you with the review and approval process.
## Large Venue Spaces
Large venue spaces should be reserved at least two months in advance. Some spaces, particularly large venues, may be reserved between a semester and a year prior to an event date — it is never too early to begin the planning process. Verify space availability before you get too far into the event planning process, and review all cancellation policies in advance to avoid incurring fees if your group can no longer move forward.
## Accessibility
We encourage you to review the [Guidelines for Accessible Meetings and EventsLinks to an external site.](https://accessibility.umn.edu/what-you-can-do/host-accessible-events) provided by the [Disability Resource CenterLinks to an external site.](https://disability.umn.edu/) when selecting and setting up your venue. This will help your student group ensure it is being as inclusive as possible for all potential attendees and group members.

### [**Example Grant:**Links to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saoexamplegrant)
**Assessing Needs:** After getting more information about the Mississippi Room prior to booking, the group decides that they should be making their tacos in a kitchen rather than multiple mini cooking stations in the Mississippi Room like they originally planned. They want to make sure that this change has as little of an impact as possible on the goals and budget for this event, so they have decided to reserve the [SGRC KitchenLinks to an external site.](https://sua.umn.edu/student-group-resource-center) for free for the same date. Their new plan is to cook the tacos as a group with the chef in the kitchen, and transport the food to the Mississippi Room for guests. This will also still allow the group to meet the goals of their event while still adjusting to the venues available to them. 
**Major Events**: After talking to their student group services advisor, the Taco Night is not considered a major event due to the 150 attendees and no high profile guest, so the group will not need additional approval from the University for this event.
**Large Venue Spaces**: The group is planning well before the 2 month cutoff for the large venue space, so the group anticipates that they should be able to find a large venue like the Mississippi Room that they are planning on for this event. Just in case that does not work, they have come up with a list of other spaces that could also meet their event needs. 
**Accessibility**: The group plans to reach out to the [Disability Resource CenterLinks to an external site.](https://disability.umn.edu/) for advice with their event after confirming funding.
