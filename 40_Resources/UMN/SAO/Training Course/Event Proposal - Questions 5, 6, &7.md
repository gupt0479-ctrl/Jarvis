# Event Proposal - Questions 5, 6, &7
## One-Line Answer
Show how your event ==benefits the campus community==, document your planning progress and to-dos, and detail how you'll promote to a genuinely campus-wide audience.
## **Question 5:**
Q5: Please explain how the event:
- Promotes student development, enhances diversity, equity, and inclusion, and/or benefits the campus community?
Resources to consider:
- SAO Grant Policy

This question asks groups to consider the larger impact of their event on the University of Minnesota - Twin Cities campus. As a minimum requirement for all SAO Grants, events must promote student development, enhance diversity, equity and inclusion, and/or benefit the campus community. 

### Answers to this question are largely dependent on the parameters of the event.

- If attending a **conference**: How are you sharing that experience with other students after returning to campus in order to promote student development?
    - Please be aware that groups are required to have a plan to share their conference knowledge after they return in order to be considered for funding. For more information, please see the [SAO Grant policiesLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saograntpolicies).
- It is important to note that not all events will contribute to the campus community in the same way and each event does not need to accomplish all of the listed forms of impact.
    - **Best practice** is to focus on the area that aligns with your event best, and directly address the impact.

## **Question 6:**
Q6: Describe your event planning process in detail.
- What steps have been confirmed/booked/reserved and which steps still need to be completed in order for this event to be
successful?
- Please include the event logistics involved, such as reservations, permits, etc. Also, specify whether or not these have been
submitted or are anticipated at the point of application.
Resources to consider:
- Major Event Policy
- The Office of
Classroom Management

The Student Activities Office aims to support groups in their event planning and event execution. Each group has an assigned [Student Group Services AdvisorLinks to an external site.](http://z.umn.edu/studentgroupadvising), that will be reviewing your event proposal. In this section, they are able to better understand steps and considerations your group has already taken, and can have a better understanding of necessary support. 

### Questions to consider when answering this question.

- If groups are partnering on this event, how is each group contributing to the planning?
- What steps have you taken to secure funding and made sure that you are on top of the requirements?
- What meetings have you had to plan and what was accomplished?
- What is still on your group's to-do list for this event?

## **Question 7:**
Q7: Describe your promotional and publicity plan to reach your target audience.
- If this grant is for conference attendance (group or individual) please describe how you will share what you learned broadly.
- If your audience is for all UMN Students, please be specific when explaining how any student would be welcome and interested in attending your event.
Resources to
consider:
- SAO Grant Policy
about "for all
student"
requirements

In order to be considered "for all students" when determining [funding eligibilityLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saogranteligibility), your group must detail specific steps that are being made to publicize your event to various groups of students across campus.

This determines your group’s eligibility for specific [SAO Grant InitiativesLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saogranteligibility). These initiatives are the different sources of funding for each grant. Each have different requirements for the event and group, so it is important to detail promotional plans if your group hopes to be eligible for all initiatives. For more information, please see our [SAO Grant PoliciesLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saograntpolicies).
