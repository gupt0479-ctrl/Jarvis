# Before You Host Your Event - Pre-Event Requirements
## One-Line Answer
Within 2 weeks of an award, submit the correct ==Statement of Agreement (RSO vs CLP)== and schedule an advisor meeting if required, or the grant is forfeited.
## **Within 2 weeks after the** [**application deadline,** Links to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/SAOgrantdeadlines)**committee decisions are sent via email**. 

Primary and secondary applicants will receive the result of their application one week after the SAO Grants committee meeting. Dates of these meetings and expected messages can be found on our deadlines resource. 

---

### **If your application has been denied:** 

Student Activities Office Grants does not have an appeals process. We encourage your group to reapply in the next cycle if their event aligns with the [next cycle deadlinesLinks to an external site.](http://z.umn.edu/saograntdeadlines). For assistance, reach out to the SAO Grants Team or make an appointment with your [student group services advisorLinks to an external site.](http://z.umn.edu/studentgroupadvising).

---

### **If you application has been awarded:**

Within 2 weeks of receiving the award message:

- #### **Student groups must submit a Statement of Agreement for each grant that they receive.**
    

- This agreement details the expectations that student groups will be held to in the SAO Grants process and is specific to each grant that is awarded. Failure to submit this agreement within two weeks of receiving the award message with result in the grant being forfeited. 
- There is a different [RSO Statement of Agreement FormLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/rsostatementofagreement) and [CLP Statement of Agreement FormLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/clpstatementofagreement). Please make sure that you are signing and submitting the correct form for your group. 

- #### **Some groups must schedule a meeting with their Student Activities Advisor.** 
    

- Review your awards message. If your group is required to meet with your Student Activities Advisor, it will be listed under "Per-Event Requirements."
- There are a few reasons that groups may be required to schedule a meeting

- New applicants will be required to schedule a meeting
- Large scale events will be required to schedule a meeting
- The committee may require student groups to schedule a meeting with their Student Activities Advisor. If this is the case, there will be a specific reason for the added contingency of a meeting listed in the "Committee Feedback" sheet of the shared proposal. 

- **Any additional requirements set by the SAO Grants Committee or the SAO Grants Team will be communicated in your grant award notification.**
