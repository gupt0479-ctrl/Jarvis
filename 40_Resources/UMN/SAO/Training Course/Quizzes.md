# Quizzes
Record of every Knowledge Check question hit so far in the 2026-2027 SAO Grants Training Course, with the confirmed correct answer for each. Primary (and secondary, where required) applicants need 90%+ overall with all quizzes submitted.
## Quiz 1: Event Planning
### Q1 — Multiple Choice: How did Gopher Cooking Club #9999 adjust their event when they learned they couldn't cook directly in the Mississippi Room?
**Correct:** ==They made the cooking portion of their event for their group members only, and booked the SGRC kitchen.==
See [[Event Venue]].
### Q2 — True/False: It is recommended that all groups appoint an access coordinator for their event.
**Correct:** ==True==
See [[Event Accessibility]].
### Q3 — Multiple Answer: How does a well-developed budget help your group?
**Correct (all four):**
- Provides data for a debrief after your event and adapt for future planning
- Improves decision-making by delegating spending authority and maintaining accountability
- Monitors your group's financial situation throughout the planning and execution process
- It helps to design and achieve your event goals by identifying what resources are needed
See [[Event Budget]].
### Q4 — Multiple Answer: 3 main decisions your group needs to make before planning your event
**Correct:**
- Total budget available
- Date or date range you would like to host the event
- Goals of your event
	*Not selected: Specific venue, Accessibility Concerns — those come later in the planning process.*
See [[Event Planning]].
## Quiz 2: Before you Apply
### Q1 — Multiple Choice: What is considered a complete SAO Grant proposal?
**Correct:** ==All required questions are answered and match the budget, the budget is balanced and includes all expenses.==
See [[Budget Proposal]].
### Q2 — True/False: The event and budget proposal should only include information about expenses your group is requesting funds for.
**Correct:** ==False==
The budget must include ALL expenses, even ones SAO Grants isn't funding. See [[Budget Proposal]].
### Q3 — Multiple Answer (3 points): What are the three areas of eligibility that every group must consider?
**Correct:**
- Event Eligibility: Special event, takes place within allowable dates, etc.
- Expense Eligibility: Consider allowable and unallowable expenses
- Group Eligibility: Registered on Gopherlink, eligible primary applicant, etc.
	*Not selected: Student Group Services Advisor prior approval of application, Bank statement eligibility — neither is a real eligibility category.*
See [[Pre-Application - Determining Funding Eligibility]].
### Q4 — True/False: It is best practice to anticipate changes when estimating costs for the budget proposal.
**Correct:** ==True==
Build a buffer into each requested expense. See [[Event Proposal - Question 2]].
## Quiz 3: Awarded Grant Requirements
### Q1 — Multiple Answer: What is each receipt required to include?
**Correct:**
- Vendor Name
- Total Cost
- Price of each item
- Date of Transaction
- Itemized list and quantity of what was purchased
- Proof of payment
	*Not selected: Initial quote prior to purchase, Method of purchase (Online vs. In-store).*
See [[FAQ - Additional Questions]].
### Q2 — True/False: You should not submit your post-event evaluation until you have all event receipts, even if it means missing your post-event deadline.
**Correct:** ==False==
Deadlines are firm. If documentation is missing, submit on time with a note on what's outstanding and why, and loop in the Grants Team or your Student Group Services Advisor — don't miss the deadline. See [[After Your Event - The Post-Event Process]].
### Q3 — Ordering: tasks from award notification to a completed grant (missed on first attempt — confirmed correct order below)
1. Grant Award Notification Received by Group
2. Group reviews pre-event requirements listed in the award notification
3. Group submits their completed Statement of Agreement based on their group type (RSO vs. CLP)
4. If required, group schedules and completes a pre-event meeting with their student group services advisor
5. Group purchases all necessary event materials, spaces, promotions, etc. as listed in the event proposal
6. Group executes event as planned
7. Group submits their post-event submission within 30 days of their event
8. Group's post-event submission is reviewed by their student group services advisor
9. Communication sent to group by their student group services advisor detailing the result of their post-event evaluation
10. SAO Grant Is Complete!
See [[Before You Host Your Event - Pre-Event Requirements]] and [[After Your Event - The Post-Event Process]].
### Q4 — Multiple Choice: Where should groups look to find all of their pre-event and post-event deadlines and requirements?
**Correct:** ==Award notification==
See [[Awarded Grant Requirements and Event Preparation - Overview]].
