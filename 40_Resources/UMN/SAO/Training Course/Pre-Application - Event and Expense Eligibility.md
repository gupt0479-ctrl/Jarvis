# Pre-Application - Event and Expense Eligibility
## One-Line Answer
==Alcohol, gambling/raffles, meetings, recruitment, and fundraising events== are automatic denials, and even eligible expenses need rent-vs-buy, single-use, and cost-basis justification.
## Determine if your Event and Event Expenses are Eligible for funding:

If you have determined that your group is eligible for funding, you can move forward to your specific event and expense eligibility. Events may only be funded by SAO Grants once, so it is important that everything about your event is included in your event proposal.

---

**Event Eligibility**:

There are a few event aspects that can **immediately remove** your event eligibility and cause your grant application to be denied.

1. It includes, or is hosted in a location, that offers alcohol.
2. It includes gambling. 

3. Please be aware that this includes “raffles” since all raffles require those that enter to pay. If attendees are not required to pay to enter, it is actually called a “prize drawing”.

4. It is a meeting, rehearsal, practice, or another form of operational gathering.
5. It is being hosted for the purpose of recruitment or fundraising
6. It violates [University PolicyLinks to an external site.](https://policy.umn.edu/).

**Expense Eligibility**:

Things to include in your proposal about your requested items:

- **Is your group planning to rent or buy this item?**

- **How will it be used during your event?** 

1. If there is a specific time that this will be used, it should be included in your group’s event schedule (question #4 in the event proposal).

- **If you are purchasing items, is it single use?**

1. Please be aware that reusable items may be considered operational items by the committee as they could be added to a group’s inventory to be used at a later date.  

- **How did you land on this amount of funding to fund this item?**

1. Your group does not need to give an exact cost to the penny in your proposal. It is helpful to the committee to understand how you came to your cost estimation, however we do recommend making an educated guess that anticipates cost change. Any unused funds will be reimbursed as a part of the post-event evaluation. There will be more information on this process later in the course.

---

### **Common Items that are not eligible for funding:**

Please note that all determinations of expenses are at the discretion of the SAO Grants Committee and Grants Team. Precedents set by previous committees do not carry over into upcoming years. This may mean that awarded expenses from previous years may not be awarded this year.  

- Events held as fundraisers for the purpose of generating revenue for the student group or outside organization
- Items that are reusable and can be kept in the student group’s inventory after the event
- Prizes or giveaways

- Examples include: gift cards, thank you gifts, and participation prizes.

- Events in which alcohol is available 
- Stipends or honoraria for any University of Minnesota student, faculty or staff member
- Weekly/biweekly occurrences, group meetings, practices, rehearsals or officer training
- General operating costs and group supplies
- Events solely intended for recruitment 
- Partisan political organizations
