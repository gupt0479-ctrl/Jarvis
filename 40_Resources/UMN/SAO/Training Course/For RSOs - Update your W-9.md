# For RSOs - Update your W-9
## One-Line Answer
RSOs must keep a current IRS W-9 on file (==legal name, tax classification, EIN, signature==) to avoid funding delays.
## **Requirement for Registered Student Organizations (RSOs):**

### **Create or update your group's [W-9Links to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/EINW9Instructions)**

All Registered Student Organizations (RSOs) are now required to submit a [Internal Revenue Service (IRS) W-9 FormLinks to an external site.](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-9 "Internal Revenue Service") to help avoid delays with funding. To make sure that your group has a completed and up to date W-9 before applying, check the following areas of the form:

---

**Name**: Enter your group's legal name on the "Name" line and any "doing business as" (DBA) name on the "Business name/disregarded entity name" line. 

**Federal Tax Classification**: Check the appropriate box that applies to your group

- **Unincorporated Association** (most groups): Check the "Other" box and specify unincorporated association
- **Corporate Nonprofits**:
    - If your organization has not received federal tax-exempt status, check the "Other" box and specify "nonprofit corporation"
    - If your organization **has** received federal tax-exempt status, check the "Other" box and specify "Tax-Exempt Organization". Also check the box for "Exempt Payee".

**Address**: Enter a mailing address where you would like to receive tax information, if there is any to send. This address can be changed by submitting a new W-9 on behalf of your group.

**Part I:** enter your group's EIN that you received through filing Form SS-4.

**SIGN AND DATE**
