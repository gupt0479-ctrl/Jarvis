## One-Line Answer
List every expense with research-backed justification and ==a cost buffer built into the estimate==, since prices change and you won't get a second expense line later.

### **In this question your group should include all of the following:**

- Amounts of **all event expenses** along with justification as to why they are necessary for the event.
- **What** you are requesting funding for
- **Why** that specific amount
- **Why** this is necessary for your event

#### **More specifically:**

- Evidence of some research done to reach the request amount for all event expenses.
- Specification of all event supplies, decorations and promotional materials.

- For more information on what can and cannot be funded, please see our [Allowable and Unallowable Expense DocumentLinks to an external site.](http://z.umn.edu/unallowableexpenses) as well as our [SAO Grant PoliciesLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saograntpolicies). 

- Designation on whether the group is **purchasing** or **renting** supplies and decorations used for the event.
- If your group has multiple sources of funding, please designate what expenses have already been covered.

**All expenses listed in Question 2 must match the expenses listed in the Budget tab of your proposal**. 

---

#### **IMPORTANT NOTE:** It is always best practice to add a buffer to the amount your group requests for an expense. 

The specific amount your group requests may be based on research that you include in your application. However, the amount you request should be an amount in which your group believes you will need to accomplish the event described. **For example**, a group that would like to provide Canes as dinner for their event attendees can say they have reviewed the menu, and believe it will cost $300 to feed 15 people dinner. This would answer the question of "why that specific amount" that is listed above. 

Groups should also expect some changes to occur during the planning process. Prices change, groups may get new information about event requirements, etc. There should be enough of a buffer built into your requested expenses to anticipate some fluctuation. **This should not be a separate expense item**, all funding does need to be assigned to an expense. These buffers should be included in your estimation of costs for specific expenses. Best case scenario, your group returns funding you did not end up needing for your event after your post-event evaluation.
