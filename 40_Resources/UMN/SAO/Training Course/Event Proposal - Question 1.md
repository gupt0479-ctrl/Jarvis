# Event Proposal - Question 1
## One-Line Answer
Fully explain your event's purpose, date, location, and how it differs from a regular group gathering - ==the committee only knows what you explicitly write==, not what you assume they'll infer.
## Question 1: Please describe your event in detail:
QI: Please describe your event in detail.
- Please make sure that you are describing your entire event, including date, location, attendance, etc.
- Be sure to clarify how your event differs from your operational events
- Please state the desired outcome (purpose) of your event
- If you are attending a conference, please include how this conference is related to your educational development.

### **In this question, your group should fully explain all aspects of the event, including but is not limited to:**

- #### **The purpose of your event**
    
    - The desired outcome and/or goals of your event.
- #### **A date and location your group intends to host your event**
    
    - Both can be changed with approval from the Grants team after your grant has been awarded. It is important to note that moving it from an on-campus event to an off-campus event will result in a reduction of eligible funding, and a reimbursement may be required. 
    - We encourage groups NOT to book a venue until their grant funding has been awarded. In the event that your grant is denied or forfeited, your group may still be required to pay fees associated with this booking. 
- #### **An explanation of why this vent is different from a group operational gathering.**
    
    - This step is especially important for group member events. 
        
- **Conference Requirement for Funding:**
    - Groups that are attending a conference must dictate how they plan to share the information they learn at the conference with campus. 

---

**It is important to understand that the committee actively uses viewpoint neutrality when reviewing all SAO Grant applications. This means that they work not to infer information that is not explicitly included in the application. If you want the committee to know something about your group or other context important to your event, you need to include it in your application. This is especially important in this question, as it is an opportunity to explain to the committee the full context of your event.**
