# After Your Event - The Post-Event Process
## One-Line Answer
Submit the Post-Event Evaluation Form (receipts, balanced post-event budget, event description) ==within 30 days== or your grant is forfeited and funding must be returned.
## Within 30 days after your group's event:

Your group is required to submit the [**Post-Event Evaluation Form**Links to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/posteventeval)**.** 

This evaluation form is used by your Student Activities Advisor to ensure that your SAO Grant funding was used in the way that was approved. They will be looking at results of your event, all event receipts, and a completed post event budget. If they find any issues with your evaluation form, or deem that you group must reimburse part or all of your grant, the primary and secondary applicants will be informed via email. 

---

### This form includes:

- #### **Copies of all event expense receipts.** 
    

- These receipts must comply with our [Acceptable Forms of Documentation GuidelinesLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/SAOgrantreceipts)

- #### **A Post-Event Budget**
    

- This is located on the final sheet of your proposal and should include all expenses and income for your event(s)
- This budget should also be balanced. The only instance in which it should not be balanced is if you were over funded for your event. If your group needed to supplement funding to cover costs, that should be included in the budget line item: "group contribution."

- #### **A description of your event**
    

- This is included in the Asana form.
- Includes: attendance numbers, outcome (whether or not it met expectations of the group), as well as what you learned from hosting the event.  
- If your group(s) received additional funding for your event, you will also need to submit documentation of that income. 

---

For more information about what is included in this form, please visit our [websiteLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/applyforSAOgrants). 

---

### All Student Activities Office (SAO) Grant funding is tentative until the post event process is complete.

Until the Student Activities Advisor ensures funding was used in the way it was applied for, groups can still have their award forfeited for failing to meet deadlines.

Failure to submit this evaluation prior to the 30 day deadline will result in your grant being forfeited. This will require your group to return all SAO grant funding.
