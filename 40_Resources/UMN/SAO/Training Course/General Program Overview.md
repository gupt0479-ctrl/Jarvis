# General Program Overview
## One-Line Answer
SAO Grants runs in four stages - ==Pre-Application, Application, Pre-Event, Post-Event== - and most of your actual work happens in Pre-Application.
## **SAO Grants Program Overview:**

Student Activities Office (SAO) Grants is a grants program run through the Student Activities Office. This program can be utilized by registered student groups on the University of Minnesota - Twin Cities campus to assist in funding their special events.

---

### **There are four main parts of the SAO Grants Program:**

- #### **Pre-Application:**
    

- Tasks and other things to consider when preparing to apply for SAO Grants. This is where the majority of student groups’ work takes place. This includes group eligibility, event planning, funding eligibility considerations, and allowable expenses that can be covered by SAO Grants.

- #### **Application:**
    

- How to submit a completed and eligible application. This includes making sure groups are submitting all necessary information.

- #### **Pre-Event:**
    

- Before you host your awarded event, there are additional requirements for groups to complete. 
- This also includes best practices to ensure that the group's post-event process goes well.

- #### **Post-Event:**
    

- This is the process each grant goes through to confirm that SAO Grant funding was used correctly. Groups undergo a review of receipts and documentation by their Student Group Services Advisor, who will determine if a reimbursement is needed.

---

### **In this course:**

We will go through each of these stages with requirements and recommendations for every student group to follow and will follow an example student group and their decisions. Some recommendations or requirements are different for Registered Student Organizations (RSOs) than Campus Life Programs (CLPs). However, the overall process remains the same for all student groups.
