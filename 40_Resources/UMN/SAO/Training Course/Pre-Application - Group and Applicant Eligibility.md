# Pre-Application - Group and Applicant Eligibility
## One-Line Answer
Your group must be in ==good standing (not probation/expired)== on Gopherlink, and both applicants must be listed officers who've completed this course with a 90%+ grade.
## **Determine if you and your group are eligible for funding:**

There are three areas of eligibility that each group must consider before applying for SAO Grants. The first area that we are going to look at is group eligibility. Learn more about each of these student group requirements in the [Student Group Policy HandbookLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/sghandbook).

---

### Group Eligibility:

- Log into [GopherlinkLinks to an external site.](http://gopherlink.umn.edu/) and search your group by clicking on the top section labeled "groups". Below your name and group number, you will see the current classification of your group.
- Groups that are on [**probation**Links to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/sghandbook) will see the word "Probation" before their group type (RSO or CLP). 
- Groups that are [**expired** Links to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/sgannualregistration)will see the word "Expired" before their group type (RSO or CLP)
- Groups that are **in** **good standing** will not see anything before their group type (RSO or CLP). Only these groups are eligible for SAO Grants.
- **Groups must be currently registered as a Registered Student Organization (RSO) or Campus Life Program (CLP) at the time of application.** 
    - **Exception**: Currently registered groups that have submitted the annual registration form in Gopherlink prior to applying are eligible. However, these groups must be fully registered prior to their first SAO Grant funded event date, or by September 30, 2026, whichever comes first. 
        - Failure to be fully registered by these deadlines will result in grant forfeiture. Delays in registration may impact funding timelines.

### Primary and Secondary Applicant Eligibility**:**

- All primary and secondary applicants must complete this course prior to applying 
    - All quizzes must be completed and you must have an overall grade of 90% or better.
- All primary and secondary applicants must be listed as an officer of their group on [GopherlinkLinks to an external site.](http://gopherlink.umn.edu/). 

**These requirements are checked by the SAO Grants Team prior to the application being sent to the committee. If it is determined that the group or applicants are not eligible, the grant is immediately denied and will not be seen by the committee.**
