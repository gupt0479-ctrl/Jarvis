# Budget Proposal
## One-Line Answer
Every expense goes in the budget tab - ==not just what SAO Grants covers== - and it must balance and match Question 2, or it's denied before committee review.
## Event Budget Proposal

The proposed budget is in the second tab of the proposal template is essentially an estimate of expenses and funding for your event. This must be completed and balanced to show the committee that all expenses your group anticipates for your event are covered with anticipated income. This must include all group contributions, other sources of funding, and ticket sales at the event. It is important that this budget matches the proposal, and accounts for every aspect of the event; even what SAO Grants cannot fund. For assistance with your budget, please reach out to the Grants Team, or schedule a meeting with your [Student Group Services AdvisorLinks to an external site.](http://z.umn.edu/studentgroupadvising). 

---

### The Budget Proposal Tab should include:

- All potential income from the event
- The expenses and income sections of your budget are balanced and that bottom right cell in the table turns green and states "Your budget is balanced, total expenses match total income".

- Proposals with an unbalanced budget will be immediately denied prior to being seen by the SAO Grants Committee. For more information, please review our [policiesLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saograntpolicies).

- Your groups budget contains ALL expenses, even if they are not being covered by the Student Activities Office Grant.
- This budget must match the expenses listed in Question 2 of your proposal.
