# Event Proposal - Questions 8 & 9
## One-Line Answer
Answer Q8 (partnership responsibilities) only if partnering - ==leaving it blank on a partnered event is an automatic denial== - and Q9 only if applying for the Student Event and Entertainment Initiative.
## If your event requires it:

Questions 8 and 9 of the proposal only need to be answered under certain circumstances. 

---

## **Question 8**
Q8: For partnership grants:
- please describe how the event is being planned by all parties and the nature of each group's responsibilities.
- Be UERY_specific when detailing each group's responsibilities for the exceution of the event.
Additional Information:
- SAO Grant Policy
- If your group is not
partnering with another
group, please respond:
N/A

In other parts of the proposal, groups are asked to talk about each group's roles in terms of event planning, promotions, etc. Here, groups are asked to describe what each group will be responsible for in terms of the planning and execution of the event. This should be directly in line with other information included in the proposal. 

**IMPORTANT NOTE:** If this question goes unanswered, and your group is partnering, your application will be considered incomplete and immediately denied.

This question is fully optional for student groups. One of the [SAO Grant InitiativesLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saogranteligibility) is the Student Events and Entertainment Initiative. This initiative is given out for each month of events, for very specific large events. The event that is chosen will receive some additional promotional opportunities from Student Unions and Activities. 

In order to be considered for this initiative, Question 9 must be completed, and the group must report that they would like to be considered on their Asana Form application.
