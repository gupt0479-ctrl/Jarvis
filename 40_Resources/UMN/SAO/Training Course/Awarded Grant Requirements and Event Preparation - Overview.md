## Awarded Grant Requirements and Completing Your Grant: Overview  

After recieving your award notification, groups have to complete their pre-event requirements. These requirements may differ depending on the award based on the committee and advisor review. However, all requirements will be included in your award notification. This award notificaiton also includes all of your following deadlines specific to your grant.
