# Event Proposal - Questions 3 & 4
## One-Line Answer
Tie your event to ==your group's mission statement== (matching Gopherlink) and lay out a detailed time-by-time agenda showing when each requested item gets used.
## **Questions 3 and 4 of the proposal are an opportunity for groups to add context to their event.** 

---

## **Question 3:**
Q3: Please describe how this event aligns with your group's mission and goals?
- Please include your group's mission statement in your response.

Your SAO Grants Event Proposal should include all contextual information the committee will need to understand groups' event. This includes information about your group and why you are hoping to host this event. This section is an opportunity for groups to supply information that informs the purpose of the event listed in question 1.

### Best practice is to include at least the following information:

- How does this event fit in with your group's mission? 
- include a copy of their group's mission statement on the application.
    - This should match information on [GopherlinkLinks to an external site.](http://gopherlink.umn.edu/). 

---

## **Question 4:**
Q4: Provide a detailed event agenda of the proposed event:
- Please include all times, locations, and activities that will be happening during your event
Resources to consider:
- Allowable and
Unallowable Expense
Document

Question 4 asks for a schedule breakdown of your event. 

### **Best practice is to include at least the following information.** 

- What does the structure of your group's event look like?
- Include when your group anticipates using each item listed in question 2.
    - For example, if your group is requesting funding for snacks and craft supplies, when will snacks be available and when do you anticipate using the craft supplies?
- What time does your group expect to arrive?
    - Will you need time to set up and take down the event?
    - Based on the parameters of the event, will additional cleaning be required?
