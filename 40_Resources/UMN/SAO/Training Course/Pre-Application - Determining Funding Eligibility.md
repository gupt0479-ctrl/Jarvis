# Pre-Application
## One-Line Answer
Before drafting anything, confirm ==applicant, event, and expense eligibility== independently - failing any one kills the whole application.
## **Determining Funding Eligibility: Overview**

Before drafting your proposal, it is important to determine whether your group, your group's event, and your event expenses are eligible for funding. 

---

### **There are 3 areas of eligibility each group must consider:**

#### **Applicant Eligibility**

- Groups must be registered with the Student Activities Office and in good standing with Student Unions and Activities (not on probation). 
- Both the primary and secondary applicant must be listed as an officer in [GopherlinkLinks to an external site.](http://gopherlink.umn.edu/)
- **Both primary and secondary** **applicants** must have completed the 2026-2027 SAO Grants Training Course regardless of their experience with grants in the past

#### **Event Eligibility**

Groups are requesting funding for a specific event through their proposal. This event must align with State and Federal laws, University Policy, SAO Grant Policies, as well as the requirements of our funding sources. These funding sources are called ["Initiatives"Links to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saogranteligibility). It is based on these initiatives that groups can find the total amount their event is eligible for.

#### **Expense Eligibility**

Even if the group and event are eligible for funding, the specific expenses that the event requires may not be. It is important that the group is aware of all allowable and unallowable expenses prior to planning their event, and are including all necessary information about these expenses in their application.
