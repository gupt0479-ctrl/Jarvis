# Before You Host Your Event - Payment and Implementation
## One-Line Answer
Funding arrives within 6 weeks of completing pre-event requirements - keep every ==compliant receipt== and use SAO/Coke branding on all promotions until then.
### **Prepare for your event:**

#### **Receive SAO Grant Funding:**

- - Student groups can expect to receive funding within 6 weeks of completing the pre-event requirements listed in the award notification. 
    - Please reach out to saogrant@umn.edu for updates on your award's funding status.

#### **Student Groups must keep all receipts for event expenses as they prepare for their event.**

- - These receipts must comply with our [Acceptable forms of Documentation Links to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/SAOgrantreceipts)guidelines in order to be considered.
    - Failure to keep all receipts may result in your group having to partially reimburse your grant funding after your event.
    - Any issues in getting necessary documentation of expenses from vendors should be immediately communicated to the SAO Grants Team. Groups are also encouraged to reach out to their Student Activities Advisor for assistance. 

#### **All event promotions must include** [**SAO Grant Logos**Links to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saograntlogos)

- - If your group has received funding through a Coke initiative (Coke Activity, Coke Development, or SEE) your group also needs to include [Coke logosLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saograntlogos)
    -  on all marketing materials. 

#### **All events should be using Coke Products**

- - See our [Coca-Cola Brand SheetLinks to an external site.](http://z.umn.edu/cocacolaoptions)
    -  for more information on which brands this includes. 
    - Please reach out to saogrant@umn.edu for approval if your group is looking to have alternate beverages.
