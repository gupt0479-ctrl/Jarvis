# FAQ - Additional Questions
## One-Line Answer
== ==
### **Frequently Asked Questions:**

- #### **[What is accepted for post-event receipts and documentationLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saograntreceipts)?**
    
    - Receipts must include:
        - Date of transaction
        - Vendor name
        - List of each item and quantity purchased
        - Price of each item
        - Total cost
        - Proof of purchase
    - Itemized receipts or paid invoices are required. Screenshots of Venmo, Cash App, Paypal, or bank account transactions **will not be accepted**.
- #### **Can we move our event date?**
    
    - It depends. Events may be pushed back, but not past May 31, 2027 and cannot be outside of the [eligible event datesLinks to an external site.](http://z.umn.edu/saograntdeadlines) for the cycle they applied under. Groups are required to submit the [event change request formLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saogranteventchanges) **prior** to the current event date included in your application. 
- #### **Are recruitment events allowable?**
    
    - Events with the primary goal of recruiting new members are **not eligible** for SAO grant funding. 
- #### **Can I apply for funding to cover food costs at a meeting my group is hosting?**
    
    - Unfortunately, no. Meetings are not eligible for funding since it is an operational event. 
- #### **Is there an appeals process?**
    
    - No. Decisions made by the committee are final. We recommend applying two cycles ahead of time to allow groups to reapply in response to committee feedback.
