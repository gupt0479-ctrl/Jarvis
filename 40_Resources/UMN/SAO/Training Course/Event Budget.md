# Event Planning: Develop a Budget
## Why Budgeting Matters
A budget is more than a spending limit — it's a planning tool. A well-developed budget helps your group:
- Design and achieve your event goals by identifying what resources are needed
- Improve decision-making by delegating spending authority and maintaining accountability
- Monitor your group's financial situation throughout the planning and execution process
- Debrief after an event and adapt for future planning

---
## Building and Executing Your Budget
We recommend following a structured process when developing a budget for any event:
1. **Break Down Anticipated Costs By Category**
2. Venue, Food, Supplies, Promotional Materials, Speakers, Etc. 
3. This is going to take some research for options, and determining which combination of choices will allow you to both **meet your goals** and **stay within budget**. 
4. **Execute** — As you get ready to host your event, track spending as you plan and spend.
5. **Revise** — Adjust as needed based on actuals, new information, or changes in scope.

---
## General Recommended Budgeting Practices
**Create a Budget Sheet** For every event, build out a detailed budget sheet with individual cost line items. This helps ensure your group is financially prepared and can clearly communicate how funds are being used.
**Keep Receipts** Save receipts for every purchase — take a photo and upload them to a shared digital folder. Receipts are essential for filing reimbursements and providing proof of purchase.
- This is required of every student group that receives an SAO Grant as a part of the post-event evaluation process. We will talk more about this requirement later in the course, but putting this practice into effect is essential to make sure everything goes smoothly. 
**Maintain a General Ledger** A general ledger gives your group a clear picture of your overall financial health by tracking all income and expenses in one place. The Student Activities Office has prepared a simple [Ledger TemplateLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/sampleledger2) to help get your group started.
- For event planning, this will help your group to know how much you are able to spend on your event.

### [**Example Grant:**Links to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saoexamplegrant)
Gopher Cooking Club has determined the following Budget Categories:
- **Professional Chef Fee (Honorarium)**: After reaching out to a couple different Chefs for quotes, the group budgets $600 for this expense. 
- **Food/Ingredients:** The group worked with the professional chef they chose to come up with a budget of $1000 for ingredients. 
- **Event Supplies:** After making a general list of what they will need for the event, the group budgets $220 for event supplies. 
- **Venue:** Given the number of people the group would like to host, they plan to have the event in the Mississippi Room in Coffman Memorial Union which costs $250 to reserve. 
The total for this event is $2070 which sits under the $2500 maximum budget the group initially set. The group is planning to apply for funding for the full $2070 amount, and if they have any surprise expenses, they are planning to cover those expenses. Ideally, their planning and budget should be planned out enough to fully cover the event.
