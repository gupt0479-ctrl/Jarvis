# Application Process
## One-Line Answer
Complete the ==event proposal and budget proposal together== for each event you're requesting funding for - the committee reviews both to determine your award.
## Application Process - Overview  

Once you have determined your group's event and expense eligibility, you can move forward with the application process and begin your [event Links to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saograntbudget)[proposalLinks to an external site.](https://z.umn.edu/saograntbudget). This proposal must be completed for each event your group is applying for. It includes an event proposal and a budget proposal. This is what the SAO Grants Committee and SAO Grants Team reviews to determine your funding.
