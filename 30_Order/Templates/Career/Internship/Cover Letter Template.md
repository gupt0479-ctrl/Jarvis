# Main Cover Letter
==The authoritative, evidence-tagged paragraph/story bank for tailored cover letters.== Mirrors [[Main Resume]]'s tagging convention exactly (`#evidence/user-confirmed-<date>` marks a fact the human actually confirmed; do not introduce an untagged claim) — per [[Cover Letter Alteration Standard]] §1, this file is authoritative and the `.docx`/`.pdf` exports are generated from it, never hand-edited independently. A per-application letter selects and recombines fragments from here, per [[30_Order/Workflows/Internship/Application Document Preparation]]'s `draft` step — it never introduces a claim that doesn't also belong, in principle, on this master.

## How This Bank Is Organized
Three fragment types, each independently selectable — a per-application letter picks exactly one opening hook, 2–3 experience paragraphs, and one closing, per [[Cover Letter Alteration Standard]] §3's 2–3-experience / 250–350-word default. Every fragment carries its own evidence tag; a fragment with no tag is not eligible to be selected into a real letter (see [[Cover Letter Alteration Standard]] §2).

## Opening Hooks — By Company Archetype
One or more real, evidence-backed hooks per archetype below. A hook is specific to *this kind* of company, never a generic "I am passionate about your mission" line (explicitly banned by [[Cover Letter Alteration]]'s own "What Tailoring Means Here" section) — each hook names a real product, problem, or initiative pattern common to that archetype, backed by something the candidate has actually built or worked on.
