## About Me

A quick system scan.

I’m a Junior majoring in Computer Science at the University of Minnesota focused on building the systems that **power the next generation** of **intelligent software**.

_On the research side_, I work on the BOOM project, where I develop Rust-based middleware and observability tooling for large-scale astronomical alert pipelines. Systems that process and track high-volume scientific events has given me experience with event-driven architectures, structured data ingestion, analytics pipelines, and the engineering challenges that emerge when reliability matters.

_On the product side_, I developed Assisto during my internship at NSEdu. More recently, I’ve been building multiple agentic systems and applications that solve my problems. A few are:

- Arc: Self learning platform that improves with time. The platform explores how memory, context, and feedback loops can improve long-term learning outcomes.
- Jarvis: A personal intelligence operating system that combines semantic retrieval, memory architectures, and knowledge graphs to create persistent context across AI agents, tools, and workflows.

Across all of my work, I am fascinated by a _single question_: **How do we build systems that make intelligence cumulative?**

I am interested in creating software that extends human capability and transforms information into actionable understanding. _Currently seeking opportunities_ to work on ambitious engineering teams building AI infrastructure, intelligent systems, developer platforms, and technologies that move us closer to truly scalable machine intelligence.
