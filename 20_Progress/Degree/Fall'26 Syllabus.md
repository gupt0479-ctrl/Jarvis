# Path to Graduation — Fall'27 Target
Shifted from Spring'27 — the one-semester target didn't fit the real credit count. F26 picking up CSCI 4521 also changed the math for the better (closes Upper-Division Major Credits outright). Fall'27 is realistic now, with Spring'27 doing most of the work and Summer'27 optional to lighten the load or move faster.

This treats F26 as complete for planning — grades aren't posted yet, but every F26 class is tracked as `IP` and assumed to land, per [[APAS]].

### What F26 closes out
- Computer Science Core — complete (CSCI 3081W, CSCI 4061, plus CSCI 3923 and STAT 3021 from earlier terms).
- Upper-Division Credits in the Major — complete, 19/19. CSCI 4521 is what closes this exactly.
- Diversified Core / Designated Themes — complete (AMES 1201 finishes the Literature sub-req).
- Upper Division Math Oriented Requirement — complete (CSCI 5304).
- Technical Electives — 6 of 23 credits done (CSCI 5304 + CSCI 4521), both CSCI-designated.

### What's still open after F26
- Technical Electives: 17 more credits, 5 more of which must be CSCI 4xxx/5xxx.
- Writing Intensive: 1 more upper-division WI course — not resolved by F26.
- General Elective Credits: 6 more.
- Entrepreneurship Minor electives: 10 more credits.
- Distinct new coursework across degree + minor: 17 + 10 = **27 credits**. The WI gap and the 6 elective credits aren't extra on top of this — pick an upper-div WI tech elective and minor electives that double as general electives, and they're absorbed inside the 27.

### Shape of the two semesters
- **Spring'27** (~18–20 cr): most of the Technical Electives — aim for 4–5 CSCI upper-division electives — plus 1 minor elective (MGMT 4171W recommended, closes the WI gap too).
- **Fall'27**, final semester (~9–13 cr): remaining Technical Electives + remaining minor electives. Light enough to leave real room for internship/job search in the last semester.
- **Optional Summer'27**: move 6–8 credits of minor electives here instead, if we want Fall'27 even lighter or want to finish sooner within the same target.
