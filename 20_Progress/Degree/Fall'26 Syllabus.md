# Spring'27 — Path to Graduation
Goal: finish the CS degree *and* the Entrepreneurship minor by Spring'27, staying in the 18–22 credit/semester range. This assumes every Fall'26 class posts as planned.

## The math
- Degree: needs 26 more credits once F26 posts (94/120) — 20 of those are Technical Electives, 6 are general Elective Credits.
- Major: needs 18 more credits (60/78) and 3 more upper-division major credits — both covered by the same 20 Technical Elective credits, no separate courses needed for these.
- Technical Electives: needs 20 more credits, 8 of which must carry a CSCI 4xxx/5xxx designator (CSCI 5304 this fall already covers 3 of the 11 required).
- Writing Intensive: needs 1 more upper-division WI course.
- Minor: needs 10 more elective credits — core (MGMT 3001 + MGMT 3015) is done once F26 posts.
- Counting the degree and the minor together, that's **~30 credits of genuinely new coursework** still needed. That doesn't fit one semester at 18–22 credits — it's closer to 1.5 semesters. Worth knowing now rather than in November.

## Three ways to actually land Spring'27
1. **Overload Spring'27** to ~26–28 credits (20 tech electives + 6–8 minor/elective credits). Needs CSE advisor sign-off for a load above the normal cap — worth asking Jacquelyn Rupp early. Only path where the degree and the minor land the same term as planned.
2. **Add 3–4 credits to Fall'26 now**, if the add window is still open (as of this note, 8/15/26, Fall classes haven't started yet). Pulling one small elective forward drops Spring'27 to ~22–26 credits — closer to normal load.
3. **Split it**: Spring'27 (20–22 credits) finishes the CS degree only — the 20 Technical Elective credits + the 1 remaining WI course. The minor's 10 elective credits move to a short Summer'27 session. Check with the advisor whether the minor can post after the major's conferral term — if not, this path pushes graduation to Summer'27.
