## Prompt
Hello everyone.  Your final exam consists of 2 questions.  You must do both.  You may NOT use any material you have already used in assignments for parts of your answers. 

Question 1:  
The areas of North and West Africa (chapters 9 and 10), East Africa (chapters 11 and 12),  and Central and South Africa (Chapters 13 and 14), the Caribbean, Central America, and South America have faced many types of turmoil as the modern world has imposed itself  on these regions including, but not limited to:  
a.  revolution,  
b.  civil war,  
c.  economic hardship and/or collapse,  
d.  foreign military involvement, and the  
e.  establishment of long term (beyond 4 years) authoritarian  rule.  
Your assignment is to :  
Choose one country in each of the six regions listed above. (So 4 countries)  
For each country chosen, pick one of the indicated types of turmoil.  Identify an event _**and**_ a person associated with that event  in the countries you have chosen, discuss them in detail, and indicate what the impact of that turmoil in that country has had on the region at the time and down to today.

AND

Question 2:  
The contemporary world throughout the last half of the 20th century and the first quarter of the the 21st century has seen significant political (and sometimes technological) changes and challenges.  Some of these challenges have been:  
  
environmental degradation  
racial/ethnic/religious diversity and conflict  
human rights  
nationalism  
democracy  
neo-imperialism (of China, Russia, USA)  
  
Choose two of these concepts.  For each concept pick two examples of nations where your chosen concept is of significant concern.  Identify the people and events associated with your chosen concept.  Discuss in detail the impact it had locally, regionally, and if applicable, worldwide.  Be specific with names, dates, and data.

Make sure to separate each country out and each topic out into their own paragraphs.

If you would like me to view and comment on your rough draft, submit it to the final exam dropbox by Monday night.  I will review it on Tuesday and leave comments in the feedback section of your submission so you can submit it back in the dropbox by the Wednesday deadline at 11::30pm.  Please make sure to email me with the request to view your rough draft or I will assume it is your final submission.

Do not forget the following:
 
The exam length is to be  1750 to 3000+ words in length.  
You must have a bibliography of your sources  
You should use footnotes, or parenthetical citations.  A rough target is from 2 to 4 footnotes per page.    
Your turnitin score should be no higher than 15%  
Please be sure to separate and label each question.  
Use paragraphs to start new ideas or points within your questions.  Paragraphs per page should run between 2 and 4, but again this is just a rough target. Your experience may differ.  
You may use single space or double space line spacing.  
Acceptable submission formats are .pdf, .doc, .docx, and .rtf.
Proofread your work before submitting it.  
Use Chicago Manual of Style for bibliography and footnotes.  MLA is also ok in a pinch, but I prefer Chicago.  
Please visit the writing lab on campus (in the tutoring center) for help with grammar, footnotes or bibliographies  
Let me know if you have any questions via email or texting my google voice number.

Remember, detail and DATA are crucial for proving any argument.  Everyone needs both!
