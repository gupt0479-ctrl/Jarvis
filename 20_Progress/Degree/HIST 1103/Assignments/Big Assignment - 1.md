For the first part of the course we have been looking at the contemporary world (post-WW2) in the 5 parts of: 1)Europe (and all it's nations) and the USSR, 2)South and Southwest Asia(India, Pakistan, Afghanistan), 3)Southeast Asia (Vietnam, Cambodia, Indonesia), 4)East Asia (China, Korea, Japan) and 5) the Middle East (Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Syria, Israel and the Arab-Israeli conflict).  For the period of 1950 through 2024,   
_**do all parts**_.

For three of the five regions, Choose one country within those regions and briefly describe the evolution of that country from 1950 down to the present day, taking note of periods of great conflict, upheaval, or change for each country chosen.   (approx 600+ words)

For each country, note three people or individual events (in any combination) that help illustrate this journey, briefly discussing their role in the evolution of your chosen countries. (You will have identified a total of 9 topics, 3 from each of the 3 regions chosen). (approx 600+ words)
