## [2026-07-30] sync | Unison chosen over rclone bisync (researched, not assumed), sync mechanism built and tested end-to-end
- Researched Unison directly from its own manual (`bcpierce00/unison`, fetched, not recalled from training) before switching off the earlier `rclone bisync` plan.
