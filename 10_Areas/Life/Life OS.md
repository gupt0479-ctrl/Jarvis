Where the vault has no real data, this note says "unknown" and points at the tracker that should capture it — no invented baselines.

Physical timeline: **Dubai (18 May → 19 Jun) → Bangalore (20 Jun → ~20 Aug) → buffer → back for Sept 1.** Currently in the Bangalore execution phase — Dubai's job was installing the routine, Bangalore's job is running it weekly with one flagship at a time ([[03 - Monthly & Phase Map]]).

## Rules lived by (condensed from [[08 - Anti-Drift Rules]] and the OS plans)
1. No platform archaeology in daily ops — tool work only in the time-boxed weekly slot, everything else to the parking lot.
2. First task before dopamine: no phone, doomscroll, or smoking before planning + breakfast + first task.
3. One tab, one task — a setup-tab urge is the cue to return to the Today note.
4. Never Miss Twice — one miss is data, two is a new (wrong) system; after a miss the next rep gets easier, not skipped.
5. One flagship per week, scope cut on Sundays — depth beats parallel starts.
6. Evidence or it didn't happen — commit hash, screenshot, or note path; the /closeday scorecard doesn't accept vibes.
7. Buffer phase closes work, never opens it.
