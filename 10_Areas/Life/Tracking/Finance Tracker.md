# Finance Tracker
Monthly snapshot, one row per month, filled on the review day [[Bank Accounts]] prescribes ("set one day each month to review totals"). The bucket system and card rules live in [[Bank Accounts]]; the investing stance (index funds, automatic, long-hold — not trading) lives in [[Stocks]]. This note only holds numbers; if a month has no row, the system wasn't run that month — that's signal, not noise.
## Monthly formula
Income − fixed bills − food − transport − fun − savings/investing = leftover. Leftover persistently negative → cut fun bucket first; persistently large → raise the automatic investing transfer.
