# Finance Scope

This folder tracks **business/project income only** — TradingView P&L as a work output, freelancing invoices, any future project revenue. Not personal banking, budgeting, or savings — that lives in The Plan (`00_Live/Career/Finance`).

**Why the split:** the old `10_Areas/Career/Finance/` held personal bank-account structure and figuring-out-my-money notes. That was personal finance living somewhere it shouldn't. It's been removed. This folder starts clean and stays scoped to money the work itself generates.

**Rule going forward:** before adding a note here, ask — is this about money the *business/project* made, or money *I* manage personally? First answer stays; second answer goes to The Plan.

Currently empty — no tracked income yet. First real entry lands when TradingView or freelance work produces its first dollar.
