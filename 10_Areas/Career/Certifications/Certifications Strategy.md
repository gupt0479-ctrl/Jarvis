# Certifications Strategy
## Why This Exists
`10_Areas/Career/Certifications/` was an empty folder until this note — the first real cert-tracking home in the vault. Source: [[PDF's Ingestion Implementation#Certifications Strategy (Career Signal + ATS Insurance)|PDF's Ingestion Implementation's Certifications Strategy]], resolved in [[00_Execution]]. `40_Resources/CS/Links`'s `### Certifications` bookmark list overlaps this almost entirely and gets folded in here rather than tracked as a second source — see [[40_Resources/CS/Links]] for the raw links themselves.
## Core Principle
Certifications are ATS-filter insurance, not a substitute for shipped projects — only 6% of AI/ML postings explicitly require one, per [[How to Pivot into an AI-ML Engineering Role in 2026 (PDF)]]. Two recognized certs cover the insurance; anything past that should feed a specific active project (trading infra, Jarvis agent work) or it's busywork.

## Anti-Output-Illusion Guardrail
Two recognized certs (Microsoft AI-900 is the confirmed one now that Google AI Essentials dropped) are ATS insurance. Anything beyond that only proceeds if it directly serves shipped work — an AWS cert for trading infrastructure, Anthropic's cert for Jarvis agent work. If a cert doesn't feed active work, skip it regardless of how "promising" it looks on the list above.
