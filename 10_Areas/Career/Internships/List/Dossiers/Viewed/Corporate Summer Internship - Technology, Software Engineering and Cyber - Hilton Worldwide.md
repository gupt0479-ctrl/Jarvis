### Exceptional Hospitality Starts with You
The Hilton corporate summer internship program offers the opportunity to gain real-world professional experience in a global company. The program is intended to develop the business leaders of tomorrow. The internship will be in person at our McLean, VA, Memphis, TN or Dallas, TX office and will begin on June 1, 2027 and end on August 6, 2027. Interns will work full-time (40 hours per week during the 10-week program). The pay rate for internships based in McLean, VA is $25.00 per hour with a one-time stipend of $2,500.00. The pay rate for internships based in Dallas, TX or Memphis, TN is $24.00 per hour with a one-time stipend of $2,500.00.
This is your chance to be a part of an in-house Technology team that's creating cutting-edge technologies revolutionizing the hospitality industry around the world! As an intern, you will bring your technical skills to a hospitality company with an award-winning culture.
Potential Internships opportunities may include:
- Intern, Security Risk Management (Dallas)
- Intern, Mobile, Software Engineer (Dallas)
- Intern, Connected Room (Memphis)
- Intern, Digital Operations (Memphis)
- Intern, Web, Software Engineer (Memphis)
- Intern, UI Developer (McLean)
- Intern, Technology Directory & Collaboration Services (Memphis)
\*Please note that available internship opportunities may vary by location and business need
**Projects you'll take ownership of:**
- Engage in impactful projects, take part in learning and development workshops, and build connections through networking and social events with Team Members and fellow interns
- Support your assigned team through the program, which could include preparing reports, presentations, conducting research, and analyzing data
- Encourage a culture of innovation and help develop new ideas and solutions
- Utilize organizational, time management, and communication skills to effectively manage tasks and collaborate with your team
- Collaborate with your manager and team to deliver a final presentation showcasing your internship experience and learnings
### What It Takes to Make the Stay
**You have these minimum qualifications:**
- Can commit to a full 10-week program (40 hours per week) onsite in our McLean, VA, Memphis, TN, or Dallas, TX corporate office
- Sophomore or junior of post-secondary education program; or have less than one (1) year of professional experience.
**It would be useful if you have:**
- Held a leadership role in your community or engaged with a student run organization.
- Internship or work experience in the hospitality or customer service (e.g. server, front desk, volunteer, coach)
- Coursework or familiarity with operating systems, software development life cycle, cyber security, mobile and web applications, and basic networking concepts
- Interest in modern AI tools and emerging technologies, with a curiosity to learn, adapt, and leverage innovative solutions to support the strategic priorities of senior IT leaders
- Currently pursuing degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, or related degree programs
