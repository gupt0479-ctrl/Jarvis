
# Internships

Store dataview entries of everything that lands inside this folder with when was the process closed. What the next action was taken for this internship application. What happened to the internship basically - what's the tracking process at. 
> Hopefully way too many files are added to this folder - aiming for 200+ easily. those many applications in a month that's all. When the folder becomes too huge we organize it based on months. Internships discovered and applied for monthly basis.
