**Innovation Services Intern \| St. Louis, MO**
_100% Employee-Owned · Big-firm resources, local-team relationships, ONE IMEG culture_
This posting is for Summer 2027 Internship opportunities.
IMEG is hiring an Innovation Services Intern in St. Louis, Missouri to support the innovation team by providing technical assistance, documenting workflows, and actively learning new technologies that support the firm’s strategic projects. Over 10-12 weeks, you will gain hands-on experience while supporting innovation project initiatives in multiple areas of specialty.
You will shadow senior specialists and contribute to early-stage innovation tasks, including research, data collection, reporting, troubleshooting, and process documentation. This role also supports knowledge sharing and cross-functional collaboration across the firm.

**Program Length:** 10-12 week summer program

## **What You'll Do**
• Participate in brainstorming sessions and contribute fresh ideas for innovation projects.
• Assist with data collection and reporting for innovation projects.
• Research emerging technologies related to innovation team objectives.
• Present research findings and project progress to the innovation team.
• Maintain an innovation log to track ideas, issues, and improvements during projects.
• Provide team and engineer support, virtual and on-site, by troubleshooting and assisting with technology issues.
• Document processes and workflows to support knowledge sharing and consistency.
