**Job Description**
DMS is seeking a **Blockchain Application Developer Intern** to join our technical team. The intern will support the design, development, testing, and implementation of blockchain-enabled applications and digital solutions. This position will provide hands-on experience with blockchain, Web3, distributed ledger technologies, smart contracts, APIs, and modern software development practices.
### Responsibilities
- Assist with the design and development of blockchain-based applications and decentralized applications (dApps)
Support the development, testing, and deployment of smart contracts using platforms such as Ethereum, Polygon, Hyperledger, or similar technologies
- Assist with integrating blockchain services with web and mobile applications through APIs and software development kits
Support applications involving digital identity, secure document verification, supply-chain traceability, asset tokenization, digital payments, and records management
- Assist with front-end and back-end development for blockchain applications
- Participate in code reviews, debugging, testing, and application security activities
- Assist with blockchain networks, wallets, nodes, and development environments
- Research emerging blockchain technologies, decentralized identity, interoperability standards, and Web3 architectures
- Prepare technical documentation, system diagrams, prototypes, and demonstrations
- Collaborate with software developers, data scientists, AI engineers, designers, and subject matter experts
**Required Skills & Experience**
- Basic knowledge of software development and programming principles
- Familiarity with programming languages such as JavaScript/TypeScript, Python, Java, Solidity, or similar
- Familiarity with blockchain technologies such as Ethereum, Solidity, Web3.js, Ethers.js, Hardhat, Remix, Hyperledger, or similar platforms
- Knowledge of web development technologies such as React, Node.js, REST APIs, HTML/CSS is a plus
- Familiarity with Git/GitHub, Docker, Visual Studio Code, Linux environments, and/or SQL or NoSQL databases is a plus
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and research skills
- Strong attention to security, data integrity, and application reliability
- Ability to learn new technologies quickly and work independently and collaboratively
- Experience through coursework, personal projects, hackathons, research, or internships involving blockchain, smart contracts, decentralized applications, or full-stack development is preferred
- GitHub portfolio or sample blockchain project is encouraged
**Education**
- Currently pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, Cybersecurity, Computer Engineering, Information Systems, Data Science, or a related technical field
