## Job Description
**Why Join our Tax Technology Team as an Intern?**
At Grant Thornton, our Tax Technology team works with large, complex organizations to improve how tax departments operate by implementing scalable technology solutions and driving meaningful process improvements.
Our work spans a range of areas, including the design, configuration, and integration of tax engines for both direct and indirect taxes. We help clients automate and streamline calculation, compliance, and reporting activities, building long-term efficiency and accuracy into their operations.
Beyond tax engines, we work with a variety of modern platforms outside of traditional tax software. This includes enterprise systems, data automation tools, workflow tracking solutions, and visualization platforms. We support clients in streamlining data collection, implementing workflow automation, enhancing tax data management, and integrating emerging technologies like AI to solve complex tax challenges.
