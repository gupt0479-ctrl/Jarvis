What impact you’ll make
As an Intern on the Power Conversion & Storage (PCS) Engineering team, you will support PCS New Product Introduction (NPI).  This role serves as interface with GE Vernova branded AI solutions, current application engineers, design engineers, and leadership to develop tools to develop GE Vernova products in the solar and energy storage product lines.
# **Job Description**
**What you’ll do (Job Responsibilities)**
- Manage the data flow and infrastructure for effective AI deployment for use with Power Conversion & Storage products
- Identify and develop data collection, document formatting and template generation to be used by an AI tool for faster engineering development
- Work closely with our AI Research Technology team to identify critical data needed to tune the AI tool
- Work closely with Engineers to develop agents and similar interface tools which meet NPI needs
- Test and program AI tools to validate their effectiveness
- Create and present training materials for the use of the AI tools created
**What you’ll bring (Basic Qualifications)**
- Working towards a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or similar from an accredited college or university
- Enrolled in a full time matriculated and nationally accredited baccalaureate or graduate program
**Other Eligibility Requirements**
- Must be able to develop and maintain good customer relationships
- Must be willing to work in geographical area specified by the business

**What Will Make You Stand Out**
- Minimum 3.0 GPA (without rounding)
- Ability to work individually and as part of a global team
- Possess excellent communication skills and the ability to interface with leadership with confidence and clarity
- Strong problem analysis and resolution skills
- Experience with AI prompt, agent, and workflow creation
