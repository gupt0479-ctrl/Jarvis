# **Job Description**
**What you’ll do (Job Responsibilities)**
- **Support** the development of system configurations for Energy Storage System applications in datacenter and utility-scale projects
- **Assist** in calculating and analyzing energy storage performance scenarios to evaluate sizing constraints
- **Research** emerging energy storage technologies, market trends, and industry standards to support application engineering decisions
- **Collaborate** with cross-functional teams including sales, product management, and engineering to contribute to customer-facing technical content
- **Participate** in technical discussions and help document site requirements and use-case needs
- **Help prepare** technical presentations, application guides, and internal reports for stakeholders
**What you’ll bring (Basic Qualifications)**
- Current enrollment in a **Bachelor's or Master's degree program** in Electrical Engineering, Power Systems, or a related technical field
- **Foundational knowledge** of electrical systems, power electronics, or energy conversion principles demonstrated through coursework or prior experience
- **Proficiency** in Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Visio)
- **Available** to commit to a full-time internship schedule for the designated program term
- **Eligible to work** in the United States for this internship without requiring any sponsorship by GEV
**What will make you stand out (Desired Qualifications)**
- You have a **passion for clean energy and decarbonization**, with a curiosity about how large-scale energy storage enables the transition to a low-carbon grid
- You are someone who **brings strong analytical thinking and attention to detail**, with the ability to translate complex technical data into clear insights
- You have **experience with energy storage, power systems, or datacenter infrastructure** through coursework, research, or a prior internship
- You are a **confident communicator** comfortable presenting technical concepts to both engineering and non-technical audiences
- You have **familiarity with tools** such as AutoCAD, ETAP, Homer Pro, Python or energy storage modeling platforms
- You bring a **collaborative, team-first mindset** and thrive in a fast-paced, cross-functional environment
For candidates applying to a U.S. based position, the pay range for this position is between $21 and $34/hr.
