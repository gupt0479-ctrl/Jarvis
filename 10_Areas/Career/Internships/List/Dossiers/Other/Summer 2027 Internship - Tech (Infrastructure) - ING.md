**Technology Internship**
ING Americas Tech is the support backbone and catalyst for new and innovative solutions that drive growth for both internal and external customers. We strive to bring technical expertise and business knowledge together to support a wide range of products within media and telecoms (TMT), utilities power and renewables, natural resources, food and agribusiness, real estate and infrastructure, with financial product specialists in industry lending, corporate finance, debt capital, commodity and export financing, sustainable finance and much more.
We are interested in dynamic and eager interns for the following departments within ING New York Technology Departments:
**This posting is focused on position in the below:**
**Infrastructure, Site Reliability Engineering & Quality Assurance**
This team is responsible for operation, maintenance, security, and development of the infrastructure components underlying business applications including the network, databases, and server components. Engineers on this team also focus on maintaining and improving reliability and quality for Americas applications and services.  The intern will assist team operations and contribute to projects.
**Typical work may include:**
- Pursue and apply methods to verify and improve the Americas asset inventory (CMDB) and dependency chains.
- Become familiar with ING Agile Way of Working and Microsoft Azure Boards to participate in sprint ceremonies and productivity measurements
- Gather Incident ticket statistics and prepare trend reports.
- Collect artifacts and prepare documents to evidence effective risk controls.
