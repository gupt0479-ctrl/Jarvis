## Job Description
**Position Summary**
- We are seeking a proactive and detail-oriented individual to join our team as a Data and Analytics Intern. This internship offers exposure to various facets of operations management and provides an opportunity to gain valuable hands-on experience in a fast-paced environment. The ideal candidate is organized, adaptable, and possesses strong problem-solving skills.
**Responsibilities**
- Build data structure and data lake intake method to support future analytics tools use at a growing Fortune 500 company
- Collaborate with team members to identify inefficiencies in operational processes and propose solutions for improvement
- Collect, analyze, and present data related to key performance indictors (KPIs) and operational metrics. Prepare reports to track progress and identify trends
- Maintain accurate records and documentation related to operational processes, procedures, and compliance requirements.
**Qualifications**
- Currently enrolled in a degree program in Data Science, Computer Science, Information Systems, Business Analytics, or a related field
- Proficiency in Python, including experience with libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, or similar data manipulation tools
- Foundational understanding of SQL and relational database concepts
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail
- Ability to work independently and manage tasks with minimal supervision
**Qualifications (Preferred)**
- Familiarity with Microsoft Fabric, Azure Data Factory, or similar data pipeline tools
- Experience with Microsoft Excel at an intermediate to advanced level (data validation, structured tables, named ranges, or form design)
- Exposure to data intake or form-based data collection workflows
- Basic understanding of data governance concepts (data quality, documentation, lineage)
- Experience with Power Automate, Power Apps, Sharepoint, PowerBI (DAX , Power Query) or other Microsoft 365 tools
- Smartsheet
- Data Pipeline development (ETL/ELT)
- Comfort working in a collaborative environment with cross-functional stakeholders
