**The Opportunity:**
We’re excited to offer**10–12 week summer internship opportunities**for students in the**George Mason Scholars Program**pursuing degrees in electrical engineering, computer engineering, computer science, computational modeling and data analysis, or related fields.
As a CACI intern, you will engage in meaningful projects and gain hands-on experience across a variety of technical capabilities. Under the guidance of experienced professionals, you’ll explore different facets of our organization, develop real-world technical skills, expand your professional network, and learn about potential career paths.
At CACI, we tackle complex challenges—developing solutions for signals intelligence, building enterprise business systems, creating analytics that drive insight and decision-making, and designing next generation systems using modern agile methodologies, cloud technologies, and advanced algorithms. You’ll receive structured assignments and mentoring, but we also value self starters who bring fresh ideas, creativity, and initiative.
Upon acceptance into the program, your**specific role, project assignment, work location, and manager**will be confirmed in the weeks that follow.
**Qualifications:**
_Required:_
- Currently enrolled at**George Mason University**and an active**GMU Scholar**
- Legally authorized to work in the United States without employer sponsorship, now or in the future
- Ability to obtain and maintain any required security clearance
- Strong academic performance and eagerness to learn
- Ability to work both independently and collaboratively
- Excellent communication, problemsolving, and organizational skills
- Professional attitude and a growth mindset
- Availability to work**40 hours per week**for**10–12 weeks**during the summer term
_Desired:_
- Interest in solving real-world problems through innovation and teamwork
- Practical experience in your area of study - be a practitioner of your trade
- Proficiency in tools/software relevant to your field
