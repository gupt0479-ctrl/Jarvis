# Machine Learning Research Engineer Internship - 2027 \[New York\]
New York
Quantbot Technologies is a leading global quantitative investment firm deploying systematic strategies across asset classes. At the heart of our approach is large-scale research powered by advanced computing infrastructure and petabytes of comprehensive market and historical data. We combine rigorous scientific methods with modern statistical and machine learning techniques to discover, test, and implement novel investment ideas.
As a Machine Learning Research Engineering Intern, you will gain hands-on experience in designing and evaluating machine learning systems that support quantitative research. You will work alongside experienced researchers and engineers, learning how to build scalable pipelines, process large datasets, and apply state-of-the-art ML methods to real-world problems in systematic investing. This internship is designed to give you exposure to both practical engineering challenges and research-driven explorationin machine learning for quantitative finance.
**Who Should Apply**
- Ideal candidates are students currently pursuing an advanced degree, who are curious, creative, and eager to learn. We are looking for people who enjoy tackling challenging problems at the intersection of software and machine learning. No prior finance experience is required, though an interest in quantitative finance is a plus.
- You should be comfortable coding in at least one major language such as Python, Rust, or C/C++, and familiar with modern ML frameworks like PyTorch, TensorFlow, or JAX. We are looking for candidates with strong engineering skills who can write clean, efficient code and work hands-on with model development and deployment while contributing effectively to the team.
- We prioritize curiosity, talent, and impact over simply checking boxes. You will thrive in this internship if you can balance independent work with collaboration, communicate clearly, manage your time effectively, and are open to learning from mentors and peers.
- Bonus points for candidates who stay current with advances in AI/ML, have experience in optimizing algorithms for specialized hardware, or have contributed to open-source projects.
**Program Details** This 10-week program runs from June to August, offering training and support with a dedicated mentor. Successful interns may receive full-time job offers.

Join a global team that values a relaxed, collaborative environment. Enjoy company outings, traditional Friday lunches, and a focus on work-life balance. Quantbot is committed to fostering an inclusive workplace where all employees can thrive. Join us for a dynamic opportunity to grow with a pioneering quant firm and acquire skills for your future career.
