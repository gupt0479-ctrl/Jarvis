# AI Engineering Intern
Frisco, TX
**Fall 2026 Internship:** This position is located at our Frisco, TX site.  Expected program dates are September 14 – December 4, 2026. Anticipated working hours will be 20-30 per week. Final hours and schedule will be determined between the intern and their manager.
**Note:** Toshiba Global Commerce Solutions will not be providing visa sponsorship for this position now or in the future. Therefore, in order to be considered for this position, **you must have the ability to work without a need for current or future visa sponsorship**.
**Introduction:**
Toshiba Global Commerce Solutions is seeking an AI Engineering Intern to help design, build, test, and improve AI-powered solutions for partner enablement, enterprise knowledge access, and workflow automation.
The intern will contribute to a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) community platform, agentic AI solutions, intelligent knowledge search, and AI-assisted workflows. This role provides hands-on experience applying generative AI to real enterprise retail and partner-support use cases.
**Key Responsibilities**
- Design and develop RAG-based solutions that provide accurate, context-aware answers from technical documentation and community knowledge.
- Build and test AI agents that can answer questions, guide integrations, support troubleshooting, and automate multi-step workflows.
- Help create an AI-powered partner community that promotes shared knowledge, reusable solutions, and reduced dependency on subject-matter experts.
- Prepare and organize documents, technical content, and knowledge sources for AI retrieval.
- Develop document ingestion, chunking, metadata, embedding, indexing, and retrieval processes.
- Integrate large language models with enterprise systems, APIs, tools, and data sources.
- Develop reusable prompts, agent instructions, tools, and workflow components.
- Support Slack-based AI workflows and collaborative knowledge-sharing experiences.
- Create evaluation methods to measure answer accuracy, relevance, completeness, groundedness, and response quality.
- Identify hallucinations, false positives, retrieval gaps, and other AI quality issues.
- Implement guardrails, human-review controls, access permissions, and responsible AI practices.
- Monitor agent performance, latency, reliability, token usage, and cost.
- Support testing, troubleshooting, documentation, deployment, and ongoing maintenance.
- Collaborate with engineering, professional services, subject-matter experts, and partner teams to identify and implement high-value AI use cases.
**Required Qualifications**
- Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Programming experience in Python, JavaScript, Java, or a similar language.
- Understanding of APIs, databases, data structures, and software development fundamentals.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and troubleshooting skills.
- Interest in generative AI, large language models, RAG, and agentic systems.
- Ability to communicate technical concepts clearly and collaborate across teams.
**Preferred Qualifications**
- Experience using AI-assisted development tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Windsurf, or similar platforms.
- Familiarity with enterprise AI platforms, including Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, or comparable services.
- Exposure to Model Context Protocol (MCP), tool calling, agent frameworks, and AI orchestration platforms.
- Familiarity with RAG, vector databases, embeddings, semantic search, prompt engineering, and agentic workflows.
- Familiarity with Slack-based AI workflows, enterprise knowledge management, and collaborative partner or developer communities.
- Experience with Git, REST APIs, cloud platforms, Docker, or CI/CD pipelines.
- Exposure to document processing, knowledge management, or enterprise search.
- Understanding of AI evaluation, observability, security, privacy, and responsible AI practices.
- Interest in retail technology, partner ecosystems, enterprise software, or technical enablement.
**What You Will Gain**
- Hands-on experience building production-oriented generative AI and agentic solutions.
- Practical exposure to RAG architecture, enterprise knowledge systems, AI evaluation, and responsible AI.
- Experience solving real business and technical enablement challenges.
- Mentorship from AI, engineering, retail technology, and professional services leaders.
- Opportunities to build portfolio-ready solutions with measurable business impact.
