We are seeking a motivated AI Developer Co-op who is currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or a related field. This role is designed for a student who is excited to apply classroom knowledge to real-world business problems and gain hands-on experience building, testing, and improving AI-driven solutions.
The co-op will work closely with experienced developers, engineers, and business partners to support AI and software development initiatives. This is an excellent opportunity for a student who is curious, collaborative, and eager to learn in a professional environment.
We are looking for candidate who can work full-time Mon-Fri in Boston, MA
## Key Responsibilities
- Support experimentation, research, and proof-of-concept work related to AI, machine learning and data science technologies
- Assist in the design, development, testing, and support of AI and data driven software solutions
- Participate in coding, debugging, and documenting applications and prototypes
- Work with structured and unstructured data to support use cases and analysis
- Collaborate with cross-functional team members to understand business needs and translate them into technical tasks
- Contribute to technical documentation, presentations, and knowledge sharing
- Follow team standards for coding, testing, security, and responsible AI use
## Required Qualifications
- Currently enrolled in a bachelor’s or master’s degree program in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, Data Science, or a related discipline
- Coursework or academic project experience in AI, machine learning, software development, data structures, or algorithms
- Basic programming experience in one or more languages such as Python, Java, or JavaScript
- Familiarity with software development fundamentals, including debugging, testing, and version control
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills
- Ability to learn quickly and work effectively in a collaborative team environment
## Preferred Qualifications
- Exposure to machine learning, large language models, natural language processing, or data analytics through coursework, research, or personal projects
- Experience with academic, internship, hackathon, or personal projects involving AI or software development
- Interest in applying AI and data science to solve practical business problems
## What the Co-op Will Gain
- Hands-on experience working on real AI, data science or other software initiatives
- Mentorship from experienced professionals
- Exposure to enterprise tools, development practices, and business applications of AI and data science
- Opportunity to strengthen technical, analytical, and professional skills in a team-based environment
