About the team:
The ONE Data Strategy team is a core function within Mastercard's ONE Data & Governance organization. Our mission is to deliver a unified operational data strategy and platform, integrating a modern Data Lake and lakehouse-aligned architecture that connects batch, streaming and operational data across multiple platforms. The ONE Data Strategy team serves as both a strategy and delivery organization focused on transforming data into a trusted enterprise asset that powers decision-making, operational efficiency, governance, AI-driven innovation, and future business capabilities.
What we’re looking for:
\- Currently enrolled in a Bachelors degree in Data Science or a related field, with an anticipated graduation date between December 2027 and June 2028
\- Experience with or knowledge of operational data platforms and observability tools, such as AIOps, Splunk, telemetry systems, DevOps tools, and Dynatrace
\- Experience applying data analytics techniques, including anomaly detection, forecasting, and data engineering
\- Proficiency in data visualization and dashboard development using tools such as Power BI, R, Shiny, or Splunk
\- Ability to analyze complex datasets and translate insights into actionable business recommendations
\- Hands-on experience with machine learning projects, including model development, deployment, or operationalization is a plus
\- Familiarity with leveraging telemetry and operational data to identify trends, optimize performance, and support predictive analytics initiatives is a plus
