# Software Engineer Summer Internship - 2027 \[New York\]
New York
Quantbot Technologies is a leading global quantitative investment firm deploying systematic strategies across asset classes. At the heart of our approach is large-scale research powered by advanced computing infrastructure and petabytes of comprehensive market and historical data. We combine rigorous scientific methods with modern statistical and machine learning techniques to discover, test, and implement novel investment ideas.
As a Software Engineer intern, you will be working with our experienced C++ team to make improvements to our core trading systems. The goal is to learn how selected parts of the trading system work and make improvements that will have an immediate impact on end-users. This position is ideal for people interested in advancing their C++ skills on an interactive, multi-faceted financial trading platform, while working closely with the team that built and monitors the system.
**Responsibilities**
- Collaborate with quantitative researchers to understand and implement their technical needs
- Develop, test, and deploy software applications that facilitate quantitative models and trading strategies
- Enhance the performance and efficiency of existing code and systems
- Integrate new data feeds into our data processing pipelines and ensure accurate delivery
- Assist in maintaining and upgrading the computing infrastructure used for research and trading
- Troubleshoot and resolve issues in live trading environments
**Requirements**
- Enrollment in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program in a STEM field (e.g., computer science, mathematics, electrical engineering). Graduates and students in their penultimate year are encouraged to apply
- Proficiency in at least one compiled language such as C++ (preferred), Rust, Java, or C#
- Proficiency in Python or another scripting language
- Understanding of algorithms and data structures
- Interest in low-level performance optimization, networking, and Linux internals
- Finance knowledge is beneficial but not essential
**Program Details** This 10 to 12-week program runs from June to August, offering training and support with a dedicated mentor. Successful interns may receive full-time job offers.
**Our Culture**
Join a global team that values a relaxed, collaborative environment. Enjoy company outings, traditional Friday lunches, and a focus on work-life balance. Quantbot is committed to fostering an inclusive workplace where all employees can thrive. Join us for a dynamic opportunity to grow with a pioneering quant firm and acquire skills for your future career.
Quantbot is an Equal Opportunity Employer and we do not discriminate based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex or sexual orientation, physical or mental disability, or age.
