Founded in 1999, **Audax Group** is a leading alternative investment manager with offices in Boston, New York, San Francisco, London and Hong Kong. With approximately $42 billion of assets under management and more than 475 employees, Audax is a leading capital partner for middle market companies, operating through three business lines: Audax Private Equity, Audax Private Debt, and Audax Strategic Capital.
For more information, visit [www.audaxgroup.com](http://www.audaxgroup.com/) or follow Audax Group on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/audaxgroup/posts/?feedView=all).
**CO-OP/Intern Cycle:** January - June 2027
**Anticipated Start & End Date:** January 4 - June 25, 2027 _(dates are flexible based on school requirements)_
**Location:** Boston, MA
**Office Requirements:** 3-4 days in office
**Team:** Business Solutions
**POSITION SUMMARY:**
Audax is looking for a dynamic, motivated **AI Engineer CO-OP** to join the business solutions team. This position will be responsible for supporting the Director, AI Solutions Architect for Audax Private Equity on AI initiatives with hands-on experience in AI development and AI governance. This position will coordinate with adjacent data teams to help create data pipelines, ensure input quality and integrity, and validate AI application outputs.
**RESPONSIBILITIES:**
- Assist with designing, implementing, maintaining, and scaling Audax’s AI ecosystem.
- Coordinate with internal IT professionals to scale AI infrastructure within the company’s cloud enterprise.
- Play an integral role in proof-of-concept development for different function area use cases within the firm.
- Assist with implementation of AI governance policies; this includes security, documentation, and privacy.
- Provide data validation and data quality assessments through coordination with the firm’s internal data engineers.
- Code within an IDE environment (VS Code) with Python, SQL, and Git.
- Utilize cloud environment services to assist with AI application development and deployment.
- Understand and experiment with modern AI architectures for experimentation and use case development.
- Support coordination across different firm function areas for information gathering, updates, and user testing.
**COMPETENCIES:**
- Experience with Python, SQL, and Git.
- Experience with the Microsoft Azure cloud environment, including GitHub, and with the Microsoft Office suite.
- Experience with OpenAI (SDKs, tools, CustomGPTs).
- Experience with Snowflake is a plus.
**REQUIREMENTS/QUALIFICATIONS:**
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Data Engineering, Data Analytics, Computational Engineering, Computer Science, or another related field.
- Experience with at least two of the following required: traditional machine learning, natural language processing, transfer learning/fine-tuning, predictive analytics, agentic frameworks, AI output evaluation, and data engineering.
- Strong written and oral communication skills as well as critical thinking skills required to operate in an efficient and meaningful way with all levels of the organization.
- Previous Co-Op or full-time experience in a related field is a plus.
**TRAVEL:** No regular travel is required for this role.
