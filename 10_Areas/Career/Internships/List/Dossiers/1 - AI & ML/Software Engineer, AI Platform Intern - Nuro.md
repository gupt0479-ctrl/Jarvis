**Who We Are**

Nuro believes self-driving vehicles are the most immediate and profound opportunity for AI to drive positive change in the physical world. Safer streets, more time for what matters, and easier access to the world around us — that's why we're building a universal autonomy platform: self-driving for all roads and all rides.

Founded in 2016, Nuro is a physical AI company developing Level 4 autonomous driving technology for a wide range of vehicles, use cases, and markets. Powered by the Nuro Driver™, our universal autonomy platform enables the global mobility ecosystem to deploy autonomy at scale, from robotaxis and logistics fleets to personal vehicles.

Nuro has raised over $2B in capital from Uber, NVIDIA, Google, Softbank, Fidelity, T. Rowe Price, and other leading investors.

**About the Role**

As a software engineering intern, you will work closely with leading experts in the field of machine learning, robotics, and software. Depending on your skill sets and areas of interest, you will work on some or all of the following: Data Platform, Onboard Systems, ML Infrastructure, Simulation, or Technical Infrastructure teams.

**About the Work**
- **Data Platform:** management system for Nuro AI Driver's data, labels, and metrics — data annotation across 2D/3D perception, mapping, behavior trajectory, language/text; data ingestion and mining via heuristics and embedding search; supports autonomy evaluation infrastructure.
- **Onboard Systems:** reliable, high-performance platform integrating autonomy software/algorithms across self-driving platforms; close collaboration with hardware, software, and systems/safety teams.
- **ML Infrastructure:** accelerator for the ML-first autonomy strategy — distributed training platform, ML compiler, model-component libraries; infra engineers work fully embedded in ML teams on cutting-edge deep learning technologies.
- **Simulation:** builds the simulator for developing/testing autonomous driving tech virtually — core simulator, sensor simulation, scenario generation, real-world + synthetic data blending.
- **Technical Infrastructure:** generic compute platform hosting mission-critical workflows (data processing, simulation), storage management for hundreds of PB of data, cloud IaaC, engineering-productivity tooling (build, CI/CD).

**About You**

You have deep expertise and prior experience in some or many of the following areas:
- Current BS or MS candidate in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Robotics, or a related field graduating in December 2026 or later.
- Experience in one or more of: backend API design, applications development, large-scale distributed systems; data storage and processing systems; advanced algorithms using C++ and Python; machine learning, multithreading; x86 architecture; software performance tuning and optimization; robotics software frameworks; different compute modalities (CPU, GPU, FPGA).
- Strong problem solving and programming skills.
