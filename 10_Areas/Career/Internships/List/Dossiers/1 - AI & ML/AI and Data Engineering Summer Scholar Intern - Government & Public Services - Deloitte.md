### Position Summary
**Consultative Offerings, Government & Public Services – Summer Scholar –** **AI and Data Engineering**
Join Deloitte’s Government & Public Services practice as a Summer Scholar and help organizations address complex challenges that matter. In this role, you will work alongside teams supporting transformation initiatives, improving performance, and helping clients generate measurable value. You will have opportunities to build consulting skills through hands-on experience, collaboration, and exposure across client environments.
Recruiting for this role ends on 9/24/2026.
**Work you'll do**
As a Summer Scholar in our Government & Public Services practice, you will be an integral member of a client service team, collaborating with diverse and talented team members to help solve multidimensional problems, improve performance, and generate value for our clients. You can capitalize on our cross-industry presence to find your niche and build your individual brand within the organization.
While a career in consulting is dynamic and evolving, we look for people who will perform in specific areas, grow those related skills, and deliver exceptional results to our clients. We recognize that candidates bring different skills, experiences, and interests, so we align Summer Scholars to skills-based profiles that support development and client impact. Setting our people up for success is a priority, and we are currently recruiting AI and Data Engineering Summer Scholars whose skills and interests align with the below description:
**AI and Data Engineering Summer Scholar**
- This Summer Scholar architects, designs, develops, and deploys enterprise solutions that span the full spectrum of Artificial Intelligence, including Chatbots, Virtual Assistants, Machine Learning, and Cognitive Services. This role combines deep expertise in advanced algorithms and scientific methods to build intelligent systems capable of interpreting data, making decisions, and uncovering actionable business insights. By merging expertise in AI, data engineering, and enterprise architecture, this Scholar empowers organizations to leverage data-driven intelligence and scalable solutions to meet evolving business and agency needs.

- Ability to lead projects or workstreams
- Ability to manage and prioritize multiple tasks in a fast-paced and dynamic environment
- Strong interpersonal skills and professional demeanor
- Ability to meet deadlines

**The team**
Deloitte’s Government & Public Services practice serves government agencies, higher education institutions, nonprofit organizations, and other public sector organizations. Teams work across strategy, operations, technology, and transformation efforts to help clients improve mission outcomes and organizational performance. **Qualifications**
Required:
- Must be currently enrolled in an accredited college or university and expected to graduate by Summer 2028 pursuing a bachelor’s degree or higher in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Computational Engineering, Data Science, Machine Learning, Robotics, or a related/equivalent program
- Strong academic track record (3.0 cumulative GPA required)
- You must reside within a commutable distance (e.g. 100-mile radius) of your assigned office with the ability to commute daily, if required
- Ability to travel 50%, on average, based on the work you do and the clients and industries/sectors you serve.
- Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. Security Clearance
- Must be legally authorized to work in the United States without the need for employer sponsorship, now or at any time in the future.
- Candidates must be at least 18 years of age at the time of employment.
Preferred:
- 3.4 cumulative GPA preferred
- Internship, project, or coursework experience in artificial intelligence, computer science, computational engineering, data science, machine learning, or robotics.
