# **Job Details:**
## Job Description:
**The Role and Impact**
Join Intel as an AI Software Engineering PhD Intern and unlock the potential of cutting-edge artificial intelligence technologies. In this role, you will contribute to the design, development, and optimization of AI software solutions, including algorithms, frameworks, and architectures across domains such as computer vision, machine learning, and deep learning. Your hands-on work will focus on implementing and tuning models for performance and accuracy, applied research, and hardware-software integration, directly influencing Intel's innovative solutions in scalable and efficient AI systems.
**Business group**
As part of Intel's global team dedicated to advancing AI technologies, you will work in a collaborative environment committed to building software solutions that drive innovation and solve complex challenges. This group focuses on creating impactful AI solutions that enhance Intel's products and services, contributing to Intel's broader mission to shape the future of technology.
**Key Responsibilities**
- Design and develop AI algorithms and software architectures for applications in computer vision, machine learning, and deep learning.
- Conduct applied research to enhance model accuracy and optimize performance across frameworks and systems.
- Implement, test, and tune machine learning models for scalability and efficiency across hardware and software platforms.
- Collaborate with internal product teams and external partners in creating system-level AI solutions.
- Learn and apply advanced AI concepts through hands-on projects aligned with Intel's business goals.
**Other Key Responsibilites**
- Strong problem-solving skills with a focus on disciplined execution and continuous improvement.
- Familiarity with advanced silicon manufacturing processes
- Ability to collaborate effectively within a team environment and communicate technical concepts clearly.
- Enthusiasm for exploring new technologies and contributing to innovative solutions.
## **Qualifications:**
**Minimum Qualifications**
- Active student pursuing a PhD in a relevant field such as Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or Artificial Intelligence with no prior professional experience required.
**Experience or coursework in 2 of the following:**
- Programming languages such as Python.
- Machine learning engineering fundamentals and frameworks.
- AI fundamentals, algorithms, and optimization techniques.
- Mathematical skills applied to AI-related problem-solving.
- Logic design of cells, circuits, and blocks.
- Design and Technology Co-Optimization (DTCO).
Take the next step in your career journey. We invite you to bring your skills, curiosity, and ideas to Intel, where you'll grow, learn, and make an impact.
