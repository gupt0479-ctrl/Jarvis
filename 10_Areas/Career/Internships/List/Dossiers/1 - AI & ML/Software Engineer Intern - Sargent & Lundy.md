## Description
**This position has a target start date in summer 2027.**
We are seeking a Software Engineer Intern to join our Full Stack Development team. In this role, you will contribute to the design, development, and delivery of web applications and services that support Sargent & Lundy’s internal operations and client-facing solutions. This hands-on internship blends back-end and front-end development with scripting, automation, and cross-functional collaboration — providing real-world experience across the full software development lifecycle.
Key Responsibilities include:
Application Development
- Develop and maintain RESTful APIs using C# .NET, following established architectural patterns and coding standards.
- Build and enhance interactive, user-facing web applications using modern frameworks and libraries such as React, MVC, and related technologies.
- Develop ad-hoc scripts and utilities to support business operations and streamline internal workflows using Python, Go, PowerShell, or other appropriate languages.
Collaboration & Delivery
- Collaborate with fellow developers, IT professionals, and end users to design, build, and integrate both homegrown and commercial applications.
- Coordinate internal customer requirements, architect programmatic solutions, execute thorough testing, and produce clear documentation of completed work.
Innovation & Continuous Improvement
- Continuously learn and present new techniques at Communities of Practice sessions to IT staff.
- Evaluate and recommend open-source tools, libraries, and other add-ins that can accelerate development timelines and improve code quality.
