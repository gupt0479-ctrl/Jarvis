# Hudson River Trading — Sophomore Internship

## 💼 Program Overview

- **Company:** Hudson River Trading (quantitative trading firm, NYC-based)
- **Role:** Sophomore Internship
- **Duration:** ~10 weeks (summer 2027)
- **Pay:** $5,800/week base (total ~$58,000)
- **Bonus:** Company-paid housing, meals, perks (no additional signing bonus for this role)
- **Who:** Sophomores graduating in 2028 (apply fall 2026/winter 2027)

---

## 🎓 Eligibility & Requirements

### Who Can Apply
- Full-time students graduating in **2028** (current sophomores as of application)
- Quantitative or technical major (CS, Math, Physics, Engineering, etc.)

### Skills Required
- **Programming:** Python OR C++ (master one well)
- **Math/Numerics:** Linear algebra, calculus, probability, statistics
- **ML/Data:** pandas, numpy, R, MATLAB (at least familiarity)
- **Problem-Solving:** Comfortable with algorithmic thinking; Leetcode-level coding

### No Finance Background Needed
- "Most HRT people joined with zero finance background" — official statement
- They provide training; you need strong fundamentals in CS/Math, not finance

## 🚨 Traps & Gotchas

### Trap 1: The $25K Signing Bonus Isn't for This Role
- You'll see "$25K signing bonus" somewhere
- That's for **Grad Hire (Algorithm Trader)**, a different posting
- **Sophomore internship = $5,800/week + housing; that's it**
- Don't get confused during application

### Trap 2: One Application Per Cycle
- You can only apply to **ONE HRT posting per cycle**
- You pick **Python OR C++ at application stage**
- You cannot apply to both
- **Choose wisely:** Python = more accessible; C++ = more systems-level (and they want C++)

### Trap 3: AI Tools Are Strictly Banned
- HRT official policy: AI tool use in interviews/assessments = instant disqualification possible
- Includes ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.
- They use multiple detection methods
- Offer can be rescinded if discovered post-hire
- **This is the strictest firm on AI:** the highest-paying roles enforce it hardest

### Trap 4: Check Domain on Offers
- Real HRT emails ONLY from **@hudson-trading.com**
- Offers never ask for banking info or wire transfers
- Quant internship scams targeting students are real
- Verify domain if you get an offer

## 📚 Interview Prep: Sample Questions to Study

### Probability & Probability Puzzles
- "How many ways can you arrange the letters in MISSISSIPPI?"
- "If I flip a fair coin 10 times, what's the probability of getting exactly 5 heads?"
- "A jar has 1000 red balls and 1000 blue balls. I draw 100 without replacement. What's the expected number of red balls?"
- "You have two envelopes. One contains 2x money of the other. You pick one. Can you gain EV by switching?"

### Coding Problems (Leetcode-style)
- LeetCode Hard: Median of Two Sorted Arrays
- LeetCode Hard: Longest Substring with At Most K Distinct Characters
- LeetCode Hard: Trapping Rain Water
- Stock trading problems (max profit with k transactions)

### Trading & Quant Questions
- "Explain what a trading signal is to someone who's never heard of it."
- "How would you backtest a strategy?"
- "What makes a quant trading strategy fail?"
- "Why should you care about latency in high-frequency trading?"
