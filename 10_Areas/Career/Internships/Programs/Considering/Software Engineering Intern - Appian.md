## Program Overview
Appian is an enterprise low-code/AI process automation software company (Nasdaq: APPN), headquartered in McLean, VA. The Software Engineering Intern role sits within Appian Engineering, contributing to production-adjacent work across the AI-powered Process Automation platform: full-stack components, AI-assisted development tooling, debugging/systems thinking, and design/code-review collaboration. 10-week program. Pay stated on the posting as $50.00/hour ($8,666/month) — `pay_per_week` above is derived from that hourly rate at 40 hrs/week, not a figure Appian states directly.
## Eligibility
Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or Computer Engineering (`eligible_classes` above); must be returning to school after the internship to continue degree requirements — that rules out graduating in the internship's own cycle but the posting never states a specific class year or grad year, so `grad_year` stays unset rather than a guessed number. Full-time (40 hrs/week) commitment required for the full 10 weeks. Considered for Summer 2027, students only.
