## Program Overview
KeyBank's Key Technology & Services (KTS) 10.5-week Enterprise Internship, Data & Analytics Track, based in Cleveland/Brooklyn, OH. Despite the source dossier's own auto-classification landing it in the CyS & Finance bucket (matched on "Vulnerability" — one of KTS's many org functions, not this track), the actual placement areas for this specific internship are Business Analysis & Optimization, Continuous Improvement, Data Analytics and Reporting, Process Improvement, Project Management, and Reporting and Analytics — a business/data-analytics program, not cybersecurity. Pays $25/hour plus a $2,000 summer internship sign-on bonus.
## Eligibility
Must have completed at least three years toward a four-year undergraduate degree in Business, Operations, Data Science, System Administration, Industrial Engineering, Statistics/Mathematics, or a related major, with anticipated graduation of May 2028 or December 2027. Minimum 3.0 GPA. No visa sponsorship is offered for this position — candidates must already have US work authorization.
