## Program Overview
LPL Financial (Nasdaq: LPLA) is seeking a Data Engineering Intern to help build and optimize the data platforms that power analytics, business intelligence, and AI-driven decision-making. The role works across data engineering, analytics, and data product teams — building data pipelines, supporting reporting/insights, and explicitly contributing to AI and Generative AI initiatives. 10-week paid internship, June 1 – August 6, 2027, based in Fort Mill SC, Charlotte NC, or Austin TX.
## Eligibility
Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Management Information Systems, Data Science, Analytics, Engineering, Mathematics, or a related field. Must be available to work from one of LPL's primary office locations. No visa sponsorship for early-career program positions.
