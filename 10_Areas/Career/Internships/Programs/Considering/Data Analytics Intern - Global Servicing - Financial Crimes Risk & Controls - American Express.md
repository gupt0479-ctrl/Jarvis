## Program Overview
American Express's Global Servicing organization, 1st Line of Defense (1LOD) Financial Crimes Risk & Controls Center of Excellence, NYC (hybrid). The Financial Crimes Data Analytics Intern uses data (SQL, Python) to identify emerging risks, evaluate control gaps, and support improvements to financial crime monitoring and reporting — anti-money laundering, sanctions, payments, and anti-corruption. Salary range $24.05–$63/hour plus a sign-on bonus.
## Eligibility
Enrolled in a bachelor's degree program in data science, analytics, computer science, statistics, economics, finance, mathematics, or a related field, with an expected graduation between December 2027 and June 2028. No visa sponsorship — must already have US work authorization.
