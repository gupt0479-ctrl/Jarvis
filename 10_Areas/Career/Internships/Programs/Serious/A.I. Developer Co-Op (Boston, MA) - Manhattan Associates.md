## Program Overview
Manhattan Associates is the global leader in supply chain and omnichannel commerce technology. This co-op sits in their R&D organization, supporting experimentation, research, and proof-of-concept work on AI, machine learning, and data science technologies that feed into their cloud-native, microservices-based supply chain platform. Full-time, Monday–Friday, in Boston, MA (not remote).
## Eligibility
Currently enrolled in a bachelor's or master's degree program in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, Data Science, or a related discipline. Requires coursework or academic project experience in AI, machine learning, software development, data structures, or algorithms, plus basic programming experience (Python, Java, or JavaScript).
