## Program Overview
Castleton Commodities International (CCI) is a global energy commodities merchant and infrastructure asset investor. This role sits on CCI's Global Data Science & Technology team's Front Office Software Engineering track (Stamford, CT / Houston, TX), building Python-based microservices/APIs, Streamlit data-visualization dashboards, and cloud-native applications (AWS, Kubernetes, Kafka) that directly support real-time trading, market-data analytics, and pricing/decision tools. Same company-level "Data Science & Technology" program confirmed live on cci.com/careers/students as the DS/ML intern track paired with this one.
## Eligibility
Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Management Information Systems, or a related technical field. Expected graduation Winter 2027 or Spring/Summer 2028. Requires strong Python (Pandas, NumPy) and familiarity with version control (Git) and software development best practices.
