## Program Overview
Deloitte's Government & Public Services (GPS) practice Summer Scholar program, AI and Data Engineering skills-track. GPS serves government agencies, higher-ed institutions, nonprofits, and other public-sector clients. This Scholar profile architects, designs, and deploys enterprise AI solutions (chatbots, virtual assistants, ML, cognitive services) merging AI, data engineering, and enterprise architecture. Posted for 4 locations: Austin TX, Boston MA, Rosslyn VA, Sacramento CA. Reasonable current wage estimate: $43/hour.
## Eligibility
Must be currently enrolled pursuing a bachelor's degree or higher in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Computational Engineering, Data Science, Machine Learning, Robotics, or a related/equivalent program, expected to graduate by Summer 2028. Minimum 3.0 GPA (3.4 preferred). Must reside within a 100-mile commutable radius of the assigned office. At least 18 years old at time of employment.

Confirm all three fit your situation before investing prep time.
