# Mimic
==Reusable message drafts by scenario. Every Contact note's Current Draft section starts here, then gets edited until it sounds like you, not the template.== This is a template library, not a contact — it earns new entries only from drafts that actually got a response, not speculation.
## Cold LinkedIn DM
Use when you have no prior connection and want a low-pressure opener, not an ask.
```
Hi [Name],
I came across the internship role at [Company] and liked how your team works on [specific thing — early-career hiring, the actual product, a recent post]. Would love to connect and learn more.
```
*Why it works:* no ask in the first message. It's a connection request with context, not a cold pitch — lowers the bar for a reply.
## Recruiter Follow-Up
Use after applying, roughly one to two weeks in, or when a recruiter has gone quiet.
```
Hi [Name],
I applied to [Role] at [Company] on [date] and wanted to check in — is there any update on timeline, or additional info I can provide? I'm still very interested in the team's work on [specific thing].
Thanks for your time,
[Your name]
```
*Why it works:* references a real date and a specific detail, so it doesn't read as a template. Respond to any recruiter reply within 24 hours — interview slots fill in batches.
## Referral Ask
Use with someone warm (alumni, past intern, mutual connection) once you already have a relationship, not as a first message.
```
Hi [Name],
I just applied to [Role] at [Company] and remembered you work there. Would you be open to referring me, or pointing me to whoever handles that? Happy to send my resume and a quick summary of why I'm a fit.
```
*Why it works:* the ask is specific and low-effort for them (a referral click, not a favor). Treat a referral as one input, not a guarantee — it does not reliably beat a strong direct application.
## Thank-You After Interview
Send within 24 hours, referencing something specific from the conversation, not a generic thanks.
```
Hi [Name],
Thank you for taking the time to talk through [specific thing discussed — a project, a team challenge]. It confirmed why I'm excited about [Company] specifically, especially [specific detail]. Looking forward to hearing next steps.
```
*Why it works:* the specificity is the entire point — a generic thank-you is indistinguishable from a template and reads as one.
