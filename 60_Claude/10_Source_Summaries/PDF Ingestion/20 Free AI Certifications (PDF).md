# 20 Free AI Certifications You Need on Your Resume — Summary
**Source:** `60_Claude/05_Clippings/PDFs/Free AI Certifications Guide.pdf`
**Ingested:** 2026-07-04
**Pages:** 15
## Source
A Maverick AI guide (2026) listing 20 free (or free-path) AI certifications from recognized companies, with time estimates and a priority/stacking strategy.
## Key Claims
- ==The value of a cert is that it's *concrete and verifiable* — it gets you past automated screening filters that scan for specific credentials, unlike "proficient in AI tools" in a skills section==
- **The strategy is stacking, not one-and-done**: get 3–5 across categories (one Google, one Microsoft, one IBM, one or two domain-specific) so the resume looks different from everyone else's
- **Priority order:** start with **Google AI Essentials** (~5h, the gold-standard starter) and **LinkedIn AI Learning** (shows directly on your LinkedIn profile), then **Microsoft AI-900** (~10–15h, appears on almost every high-paying AI posting)
> [!WARNING] Directly conflicts with a stronger source: [[How to Pivot into an AI-ML Engineering Role in 2026 (PDF)]] found that **only 6% of AI/ML postings request certifications** and that project work matters far more. Reconcile: certs are cheap ATS-filter insurance and a resume signal *for someone with no ML projects yet*, not a substitute for the 3 deployed projects that actually land interviews.
## Full Content — the certifications
**Top 5 (highest recruiter recognition):**
1. **Google AI Essentials** — AI fundamentals, prompt engineering, responsible AI (~5h, beginner) · grow.google/certificates/ai-essentials
2. **LinkedIn AI Learning** — shows on your LinkedIn profile (~3–5h)
3. (a ~3h beginner cert) and a **GitHub** cert normally $99, free via GitHub education (~8h)
4. **Microsoft AI Fundamentals (AI-900)** — ML, computer vision, NLP, Azure AI; free virtual training days include a free exam voucher (~10–15h) · learn.microsoft.com
**15 more (recognized, legitimate):** AWS Cloud Practitioner Essentials (~6h), **AWS AI & ML Scholars Program** (free, 50k+ learners in 2025, mentorship + prep for AWS Certified AI Practitioner), Google Cloud Data Analytics (BigQuery, no coding, ~15h), **IBM AI Fundamentals** (IBM SkillsBuild, official Credly badge, ~10h), plus others across Google/Microsoft/IBM/AWS.
## Why It Matters
A cheap, weekend-scale resume-signal lever for the internship pipeline ([[Tracker]]) — but explicitly **secondary to project work** per the pivot-guide's data. The honest play for a CS student: grab **Google AI Essentials + AI-900** (highest recognition, ~15h total, free) as ATS insurance while the real effort goes into a deployed Bangalore-flagship project. The AWS AI & ML Scholars Program is the most substantial (mentorship + real prep), worth more than a badge-only cert. Don't let cert-stacking become Output-Illusion busywork ([[The Output Audit (web)]]) — 2 recognized certs, then back to building.
