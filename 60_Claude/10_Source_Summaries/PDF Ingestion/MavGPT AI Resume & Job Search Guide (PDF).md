## Source
An ATS-optimization + job-search guide by MavGPT, on getting resumes past Applicant Tracking Systems using AI.
## Key Claims
- ==90% of companies use ATS to filter resumes before a human sees them — rejection is often "by a robot," for missing keywords, not for being unqualified==
- The core move is **matching the resume's keywords/skills to the specific job description** so the ATS scores it as a match
- AI (Claude/ChatGPT) is the tool to tailor each resume to each posting at scale rather than sending one generic resume everywhere
## Full Content
The guide's spine: ATS filters on keyword match → get the resume parsed cleanly (simple formatting, standard headings) → mirror the job description's language → use AI to tailor per application. (The clip captured the premise and structure; the per-section prompt templates are in the PDF.)
