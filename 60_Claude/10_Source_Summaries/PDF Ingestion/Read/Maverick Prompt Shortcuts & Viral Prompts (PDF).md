# Maverick's Prompt Shortcuts & Viral Prompts — Summary
**Source:** `60_Claude/05_Clippings/PDFs/Maverick's 100 prompt shortcuts.pdf` (+ `Maverick's Viral Prompts.pdf`, @mavgpt)
**Ingested:** 2026-07-04
**Pages:** 11 + 18
## Source
Two prompt collections from Maverick (@mavgpt): "100 Claude Secret Codes" (keyword shortcuts organized by category) and "All My Viral Prompts" (viral-mode and use-case prompts with explanations).
> [!WARNING] The "100 codes" are **prompt keywords, not real slash-commands** — typing `/ghost` or `/deepthink` just nudges the model's behavior through the word's connotation; they're a naming vocabulary for prompt *modes*, not installed commands like the Jarvis `.claude/commands/`.
## Key Claims
- ==The codes work by "forcing a specific mode, format, or reasoning pattern" — they're shorthand for behaviors you'd otherwise spell out in a sentence==
- The genuinely reusable ones are the **thinking/analysis modes**: `PARETO` (the 20% driving 80%), `/premortem` (imagine the plan failed, find why), `/redteam` (tear the idea apart), `INVERT` (solve it backwards), `/blindspots` (what you didn't think to ask), `WARGAME` (simulate competitor response)
- Viral prompts include "God Mode / Absolute Mode" personas and use-case prompts (headshots, apps, discount codes) plus psychology/self-discovery prompts
## Full Content — categories (100 codes)
- **Core Writing & Style:** /ghost (de-AI text), /mirror (match your style from a sample), /raw, /voice, /punch, /flow, /trim, /hook, /rephrase, /polish
- **Artifacts & Creation:** ARTIFACTS, /buildme, DASHBOARD, PROTOTYPE, CANVAS, /render, BLUEPRINT, WIREFRAME, /livecode, GENERATOR
- **Thinking & Reasoning:** OODA, /deepthink, L99, CHAINLOGIC, /blindspots, OVERTHINK, /unpack, INVERT, /layered (surface/mid/expert), XRAY
- **Learning & Mastery:** /teachme, GAPFINDER, /eli5, MASTERCLASS, /drill (practice exercises), SPEEDRUN, /mentor, LEVELUP, CRASHCOURSE, BOOTCAMP
- **Analysis & Strategy:** /redteam, PARETO, /swot, WARGAME, /premortem, LEVERAGE, /audit, BOTTLENECK
- Plus categories: Creative & Content, Coding & Technical, Research & Deep Dives, Power Commands, Hidden Modes.
## Why It Matters
Mostly a **vocabulary reference**, not new capability — but a few of these map onto real Jarvis gaps and confirm patterns from stronger sources: `/ghost` is exactly the anti-slop rewrite the vault's [[anti-slop-editor]] does; `/premortem`, `/redteam`, `PARETO`, `INVERT` are the same "pressure-test your thinking" moves as the /challenge command flagged as a gap in [[Claude OS]] (from both Internet Vin and second-brain-claudekit). The honest read: these are prompt-engineering shorthand a strong user already reaches for; the useful move isn't installing them but noting which *behaviors* (premortem, red-team, blindspot-finding) deserve to become real Jarvis skills.
