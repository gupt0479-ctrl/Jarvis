# 4 Ways to Make Money with the Hermes Agent — Summary
**Source:** `60_Claude/05_Clippings/Web/4 Ways to Make Money With the Hermes Agent.md`
**Ingested:** 2026-07-04
**Pages:** web guide
## Source
A playbook for monetizing "Hermes" (an AI agent) across four business functions, framed around one discipline: treat the agent as a junior operator attached to a function people already pay for.
## Key Claims
- ==Hermes is **not a money printer, it's a junior operator** — it removes the repetitive operator work *around* a real opportunity (research, follow-ups, drafting, monitoring, reminders), not the need for a real offer, distribution, or talking to customers==
- **Two rules for every play:** (1) attach it to a function people already pay for (sales, content, research, monitoring, ops); (2) **sell the outcome, never the method** — "nobody buys AI outreach; they buy a weekly pipeline of qualified leads"
- **Four plays is three too many to start** — the fastest way to make nothing is to dabble in all of them; pick one
- Each play has three parts: the money model (what you charge, who buys), the Prompt Vault (the bad version most people run + the good version to paste), and a "go deeper" build video
## Full Content — the four plays
1. **Sales — lead generation & outreach.** "The most boring one, which usually means the most real one." Hermes as a research-and-follow-up operator (find accounts → qualify → find the angle → write → follow up → track replies), *not a spam cannon*. Money model: a done-for-you prospecting system for agencies/founders — sell "a weekly pipeline of qualified leads with custom opening angles," recurring.
2. **Content**, **3. Research**, **4. Monitoring** — the remaining three plays follow the same money-model + prompt-vault + go-deeper structure (attach to a paid function, sell the outcome). *(The clip's detail is fullest on Play 1; the others share the pattern.)*
## Why It Matters
The "junior operator, sell the outcome not the method" framing is the most useful transferable idea, and it's the freelancing/consulting angle for [[Freelancing]] — a realistic side-income path that uses the exact agent-building skills Anant already has ([[Claude OS]]), without the "let an AI run my business" fantasy. It's the individual-operator version of the [[Relevance AI — AI Agents for Sales & GTM (web)]] agent-workforce model. Honest caveat: this is influencer-monetization content with a prompt-vault upsell; the durable part is the discipline (attach to a paid function, pick one play, sell outcomes) — the same "solve a real problem" priority the [[Free AI Receptionist Workflow — lead magnet (PDF)]] gestured at. Off the critical path for the summer (flagships + internship pipeline come first), but a real fallback income model.
