## Full Content — the three programs
1. **Hudson River Trading (HRT) — the quant one.** Sophomore internship, rotation through **algorithm development (quant research) + software engineering** so you see both lanes before committing. Pay: **$5,800/week base + company-paid housing/meals/perks.** Who: full-time students **graduating 2028 (current sophomores)** in a quantitative/technical major. Skills: Python, C, or C++ + stats/numerical/ML (pandas, numpy, R, MATLAB). Status: summer 2026 closed, check back for 2027. Bonus track: "inside HRT" 3-day immersive for 1st/2nd-years. *Traps:* the $25k signing bonus is on a different (grad algo-trader) posting, not this one; you can apply to **only ONE HRT posting per cycle**, and pick C++ or Python at application.
2. **Capital One — "tech-forward bank."** ~10-week paid SWE internship, opens with a mini coding bootcamp then a real Capital One project. Who: students who **finished 2nd year (rising juniors)**. Earlier option: "Tech Summit," a 5-day event for freshmen/sophomores feeding the bigger programs. *(Re-check the week count and cutoff on the live page — edited each cycle.)*
3. **Bloomberg — less an internship, more a door.** Technical-interview prep + **weekly 1-on-1 mentoring with a Bloomberg engineer**; on completion you **skip the resume screen and go straight to the technical interview**. Sibling earlier-funnel programs: Bloomberg Launch, First-Year Insights, Tech Labs.
> [!WARNING] The author's repeated caution: these programs quietly rewrote eligibility after 2023, and terms/links change every cycle — **confirm the live official page before applying**, don't trust a rumor or a stale crowd-sourced list.
