## Source
A grad-year-sorted list of early-career programs locked to freshmen and sophomores — the ones that "vanish the day you turn junior, and nobody tells you when you aged out."
## Key Claims
- ==Go by **what year you'll be when the internship runs**, not your year now — summer-2027 programs are applied to in fall 2026 / spring 2027==
- **The freshman-only trap:** Two Sigma First-Year and Jane Street FTTP are freshman ONLY — once you finish freshman year you're out of those exact programs; apply to their regular sophomore/SWE tracks instead
- Crowd-sourced GitHub lists go stale fast — always confirm the official page before applying

## Why It Matters
The companion program map to [[Fintech Early Programs That Pay — HRT, Capital One, Bloomberg (web)]] — together they're a near-complete early-career target list for [[Tracker]]. The load-bearing caveat is the same: **Anant's grad year determines which of these he can still apply to.** If he's a rising junior for 2027, the freshman-only (Two Sigma First-Year, Jane Street FTTP) and most 1st+2nd-year programs (Microsoft Explore, Google ASDI, LinkedIn First Play) have aged out — leaving **Citadel Launch (2nd-year, $4,300–4,800/wk)**, MLH Fellowship (any year), and NASA OSTEM as still-open, plus the regular SWE/junior tracks. The "any year" ones (MLH, NASA) are the safe adds regardless. Every window opens in fall — the pipeline-touch cadence in [[Weekly Operating System]] should front-load applications into Aug–Nov 2026.
