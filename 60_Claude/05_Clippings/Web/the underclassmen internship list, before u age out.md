Microsoft, Google, Citadel, and Jane Street run early programs locked to freshmen and sophomores. the day u turn junior, most of them vanish. and nobody tells u when u aged out. this is the list, sorted by who can actually apply right now.

how to read this. open now · closed, opens later so watch the date · link checked june 16 2026 · not re-checked yet, confirm before applying.

go by what year ull be when the internship runs, not ur year right now. summer 2027 programs get applied to in fall 2026 and spring 2027.
