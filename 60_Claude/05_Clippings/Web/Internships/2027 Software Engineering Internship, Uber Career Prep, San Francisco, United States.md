**About the role and team**

Interns at Uber don’t just observe — they build. You’ll take on meaningful projects, move fast, and be trusted from day one. In this role, you will work alongside a collective of doers to tackle ambitious problems, designing and developing software that impacts millions of users who rely on the Uber platform every day.

You will join a dynamic, supportive team of backend, frontend, and mobile engineers, along with product managers and data scientists. This environment is built for those who thrive in a high-growth, fast-paced setting where you are expected to take ownership of your work and contribute to the real-world movement of people and assets globally.

**What you’ll do**

- Work with your manager and mentor to drive exciting, ambiguous, previously unsolved projects from end to end.
- Design, develop, and test software applications and components using Uber’s modern technology stack.
- Propose ideas, implement solutions, experiment, and launch features that directly impact the customer experience for riders, drivers, and merchants.
- Keep the bar high on sustainable engineering by following best practices for code health, documentation, testing, and monitoring.
- Influence other engineers and stakeholders by applying technical knowledge to evaluate complex data and resolve technical issues.
- Own your work with independence, moving projects forward while collaborating with cross-functional teams across different global offices.

**Basic Qualifications**

- Actively pursuing a Bachelor’s (3rd/4th year) or Master’s in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field.
- Must have at least one remaining semester of school left after the completion of the internship.
- Prior exposure to software engineering best practices through university projects, coding challenges, or previous internships.
- Proficiency in one or more application or systems languages such as Go, Python, Java, or C/C++.

**Preferred Qualifications**

- Member of the 2026 [Uber Career Prep](https://jobs.uber.com/en/teams/emerging-talent/careerprep/) Cohort
- Experience building scalable tools or infrastructure in a fast-paced environment.
- A strong commitment to writing understandable, maintainable, and reusable software.
- Demonstrated ability to learn new languages and methodologies quickly.
- Evidence of technical leadership or initiative in hackathons and coding competitions.

For San Francisco, CA-based roles: The base hourly rate amount for this role is USD $52.00 per hour.

For Seattle, WA-based roles: The base hourly rate amount for this role is USD $52.00 per hour.

For Sunnyvale, CA-based roles: The base hourly rate amount for this role is USD $52.00 per hour.

US interns are eligible for comprehensive health coverage, life and disability insurance, and additional benefits. They are also eligible for paid time off.
