## Question 1 (2.1.002) — Freeway exit: R, L, S; three vehicles

**(a)** All three same direction:
`A = {RRR, LLL, SSS}`

**(b)** All three take different directions (one each of R, L, S in some order):
`B = {RLS, RSL, LRS, LSR, SRL, SLR}`

**(c)** Exactly two turn right (two R's, one non-R):
`C = {RRL, RRS, RLR, RSR, LRR, SRR}`

**(d)** Exactly two vehicles go in the same direction (a matching pair, third differs). Three groups of 6:
`D = {RRL, RRS, RLR, RSR, LRR, SRR, LLR, LLS, LRL, LSL, RLL, SLL, SSR, SSL, SRS, SLS, RSS, LSS}`

**(e)**
- **D'** = outcomes NOT in D = all-same ∪ all-different:
  `D' = {RRR, LLL, SSS, RLS, RSL, LRS, LSR, SRL, SLR}`
- **C ∪ D**: C ⊆ D (all outcomes with exactly two R's are already in D), so:

`A = {SSF, SFS, FSS}`

`B = {SSS, SSF, SFS, FSS}`

- **B ∩ C**: C ⊆ B, so: `B ∩ C = {SSS, SSF, SFS}`
